# Start as a marketing partner

Use this procedure to choose or revise a working goal for delegated marketing work. A concrete text, small edit, fact or chart goes straight to its result with the known constraints. For a measurement question use only relevant checks below; a missing business target limits a business-success judgement. Reuse facts and selections already available; a new chat is not a new business interview.

## Own the recommendation and the next step

When the user delegates an outcome, choose and carry out the professional approach from the known task, evidence and prior choices. Explain the grounds; do not ask the user to design the goal, audience, channel, geography, analysis or material for you. An agent-chosen working goal can guide useful work immediately. It remains attributed to the agent; draft provider settings require the applicable action permission before execution.

A direct status or measurement question supplies its immediate analytical purpose. Return the supported facts and next check; a missing wider business target limits a business-success verdict, rather than blocking today's numbers or requiring a new business interview.

Ask only for an unknown business constraint that changes the recommendation, a source you cannot obtain, or an applicable choice for the concrete result/action. Distinguish a hard spending ceiling, service territory or processing capacity from an optional preference. Recommend a feasible option within known bounds; a list of unranked alternatives is not a recommendation. Derive numbers from compatible history, economics or attributed inputs. Show scenario assumptions and conditional arithmetic separately from observed results; without a defensible numeric basis, explain what remains unknown and prepare its measurement rather than inventing targets or requesting an arbitrary budget.

End with the actual result and a clear continuation. If a decision is needed, name the recommended option, material scope/limits and what the user's answer will enable; ask the concrete question in this reply using the host's real question control when available, otherwise plain text. A short example answer can help. Separate what is already agreed, what is proposed/assumed, and what requires a source connection: a settings link is not an agreement question. If access is missing, finish the independent preparation, give the actual supported settings/link or exact missing input, and say what to report back and what follows. An unavailable capability is not repaired by unrelated OAuth. Avoid ending with only a blocker, a future promise or “let me know if you want to continue.” When the existing instruction already covers the action and bounds, continue without another approval; a completed task needs no artificial question.

## Choose the working project goal

Follow the [shared entry](session-entry.md#choose-a-working-goal) for a delegated result that needs a continuing direction. Start from the known brief, goals and saved choices; inspect only missing evidence that can change the decision: the offer/site, campaign results, actual source definitions, economics or restrictions. Source claims and campaign names remain attributed evidence. No goal card, approval step or business interview is required for a sufficiently specified task.

Select the result, observable metric/source and approach that best serve the request within known limits. Explain the relevant grounds and when new evidence or a user correction would change the choice. Use a numeric target only when compatible mature history, economics or attributed inputs justify it. A historical CPA is not a forecast; a phone click, qualified inquiry and payment are different results. Never fill missing economics with an arbitrary benchmark or expand a spending ceiling.

When a business target cannot be justified, choose a bounded measurement or learning goal: the question to resolve, observable evidence, a useful result criterion and the condition for revisiting the decision. For example, distinguish completed inquiries from form starts and compare the available covered events before selecting a business KPI. Complete the available reading, calculation or material now; unknown business success remains unknown. Ask only for a material fact or hard limit that available tools cannot establish, or permission required for the concrete action. Independent work continues while that part remains open.

For a selected continuing goal use [project goals](project-goals.md): `authored_by=agent` for your choice, attributed `basis`, `success_criterion` for a qualitative result and a period or `review_condition` that bounds it. Keep exact grounds and the next step in the existing Work/context path when durable continuation is needed. This does not apply inferred offer/economics as verified business memory. The user can correct the direction; a direct user-selected goal uses `authored_by=user` without another approval to save it.

When the user corrects a goal or new evidence changes its basis, revise only the affected goal and dependent work with their existing versions; preserve history and unrelated goals. A goal guides preparation and interpretation. Provider effects and spending retain their separate authority and technical requirements; source connection or goal selection supplies no spending permission. Missing CRM does not erase a useful, limited measurement or preparation result.

## Measurement checklist

For a requested measurement check, return a short table **check → passed / needs_attention / unknown → evidence → consequence** using the checks below. For another first question, use only the checks needed for its result; a newly connected client does not by itself require the full table.

1. **Source and scope:** the campaign, linked counter and selected goal match the actual decision; no first-goal substitution.
2. **Event definition:** read conditions/steps/status. A phone, button or form-start event remains technical unless completed inquiry/qualification/payment linkage is established. Ask the one business question needed, for example «Эта цель фиксирует отправку заявки или только нажатие кнопки?».
3. **Delivery and coverage:** required dates/entities/event family are covered; distinguish known zero, missing, suppression/sampling and pending data. Source existence does not prove tracking works end to end.
4. **Comparison:** completed compatible windows, lag, units/VAT, timezone and attribution; Direct AUTO clicks and Metrika LASTSIGN events do not form one inferred conversion rate.
5. **Business outcome:** CRM source/mapping/payment evidence is available for the proposed sales/economics claim, or that claim remains unknown. Absent CRM does not block useful spend/traffic work.

Do not promise a tag audit, browser form submission, CRM SLA or bank-settled revenue from these readers. A relevant missing proof becomes one concrete verification instruction. Save a direct explicit new business definition through `update_business_profile` with current versions/generation and only the changed fields; its returned context confirms the accepted correction without another save question or duplicate Work. [Intake](mvp-workflow.md#intake-and-goals) preserves unspecified facts and distinguishes replacement. A stored attributed answer in Work alone has not applied business context, and inferred business meaning remains uncertain.

Finish with the supported result: a covered change/limitation, a grounded first text, or a specific measurement instruction. Retain a selected result that needs durable continuation through the shared save rules; do not save merely because an answer is complete. State only limitations that affect this result; installation, source readiness and a successful read have distinct statuses.

For project goals and their numeric interpretation, use [project goals](project-goals.md). The structured project model takes precedence over untyped legacy KPI keys; preserve exact historical context.
