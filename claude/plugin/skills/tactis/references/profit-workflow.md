# Profit: economics, budget choices and revision

Use for comparing promotion economics, setting a paid-sale cost limit, planning a constrained budget or revisiting the choice after payments/refunds. Start with two homogeneous services, each in a distinct existing campaign of one authorized account. The useful result is an economic card, a justified budget proposal and its subsequent revision. Enter at the user's current question; reuse earlier facts/material and ask only for the missing input. The external agent interprets and calculates; the backend retains source facts and Work. This procedure does not produce verified accounting profit or execute a budget change.

## Read what is already known

For a comparison across actual advertising and CRM publications, use [the marketing data matrix](marketing-data-matrix.md) first. Reuse its compatible costs, explicit cohort definitions and exact source references. A blocked money/cohort ratio cannot be repaired by allocating account revenue to campaigns or treating won value as paid revenue. Preserve unknown acquisition, tax, refunds, costs and maturity; a matrix is not person-level attribution or verified accounting profit.

## Fill the one material gap

Reuse the user's available business aggregates. If needed, ask one short question appropriate to the missing input, for example: “За этот период сколько оплаченных продаж относится к каждой услуге и сколько от них осталось после себестоимости, переменных расходов и возвратов, до расходов на рекламу?” State the period and services yourself from the read context. If those amounts are already known, ask only about the missing condition, such as whether refunds are included.

For each service retain paid-sale count N and contribution before advertising C, with their source and cutoff. For a homogeneous service, an explicitly supplied actual contribution per sale × N is acceptable; a mixed assortment needs total contribution. Revenue, average order value, a technical event or assumed conversion value cannot replace C. Attribute supplied amounts to the user; do not apply them to confirmed memory/goals or claim CRM verification.

Check decision-relevant compatibility before ranking: same advertising cohort and campaign linkage, completed period/calendar, enough time for payments/refunds under the stated business lag, same RUB currency and VAT basis. Sales recorded by payment date may come from another advertising period. Establish which costs, discounts and refunds/reserves C includes; advertising must not already be deducted. Do not guess a tax adjustment, lag or refund reserve. An unresolved material condition leaves the financial ranking unknown; retain the useful supported figures and ask for that specific missing condition. Contribution can be negative. A reported zero is usable only when its meaning and coverage are established.

## Give the comparison and one next step

For compatible inputs calculate externally:

- Contribution after advertising: C − S, where S is owner-backed spend.
- Contribution before advertising per advertising ruble: C / S.
- Cost per paid sale: S / N.

Unknown inputs leave their dependent results unavailable. A zero denominator leaves the ratio unavailable; do not silently make it zero or infinite. Show period, units and provenance next to a compact table. These are calculations over supplied business inputs, not new owner-derived A1 metrics, ROAS or a native profit measurement. Keep excluded overheads visible; call C − S “вклад после рекламы”, not “чистая прибыль”.

**Explicitly hypothetical example; never substitute these values for a client's facts.** Two homogeneous services have compatible complete spend and a mature cohort. The business supplies 20/10 paid sales and actual contribution of 4/8 thousand RUB per sale, after agreed variable costs/refunds, before advertising, on the same VAT-excluded basis as spend.

| За один завершённый период, тыс. ₽ | Услуга A | Услуга B | Основание |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Расход рекламы S | 60 | 40 | Owner spend, без НДС |
| Оплаченные продажи N, шт. | 20 | 10 | Сообщено бизнесом |
| Вклад до рекламы C | 80 | 80 | Сообщённый вклад на продажу × N |
| Вклад после рекламы C − S | 20 | 40 | Расчёт агента по этим входам |
| Вклад до рекламы / расход, ₽/₽ | 1,33 | 2,00 | C / S |
| Цена оплаченной продажи, тыс. ₽ | 3 | 4 | S / N |

Explain: “По этим данным B даёт больший вклад после рекламы: 40 против 20 тыс. ₽, хотя цена продажи выше. Следующий шаг — проверить свободную мощность B перед решением о дополнительном продвижении.” Choose the next step from this client's actual constraints, not automatically from the example. Historical efficiency does not establish marginal returns, capacity or causal benefit. A recommendation is separate from an executed advertising change; a later action uses the existing Ads procedure and its current permissions and user choice.

**Useful needs-data branch.** Spend is complete but the goal is “клик по телефону”, CRM sales are unlinked and contribution is absent. Present the two known spends and the event's actual meaning; say that profitability cannot yet be ranked. Ask for one nonpersonal aggregate summary of paid sales/contribution by these two services for the matching cohort, including refunds and cutoff. Save this concrete unresolved comparison and next step. Account revenue or a zero technical goal does not establish service profit or absence of paid sales. If one service has suitable inputs, show its conditional calculation while keeping the other and their ranking unknown.

## Economic card: a paid-sale cost limit

Reuse the comparison inputs; ask only for the desired remaining contribution t per paid sale after advertising when it is unknown: “Сколько хочешь оставлять с одной оплаченной продажи после рекламы?” The user may choose a different t for each service. This is an attributed business choice, not automatically a confirmed goal or a provider target. Do not default t to zero; an explicit break-even request can establish that choice.

Calculate m = C / N, the contribution per paid sale before advertising; P = m − t, the economic paid-sale cost limit; a = S / N, the historical average advertising cost per paid sale; and h = P − a, the headroom under these inputs. Retain input origins, definitions, period/cohort/cutoff, money basis and material limitations in the card. For N=0 or unknown, derived m/a/h remain unavailable; an explicitly supplied actual m can support a conditional P without a current-price comparison. Mixed or unknown money/cohort definitions block the financial comparison. If P=0, positive advertising costs would not meet t in this model; if P<0, even zero advertising cost would not reach t. A negative limit is not a zero setting to apply.

In the hypothetical comparison above, chosen t of 1/2 thousand RUB yields m=4/8, P=3/6, a=3/4 and h=0/2 thousand RUB for A/B. Explain: “A на выбранной границе; у B запас 2 тыс. ₽ на продажу по этим данным.” The limit concerns a historical average with supplied costs, not the marginal cost of another sale. Technical-event CPA, qualified-lead CPA and paid-sale CPA are different. Do not turn P into TARGET_CPA, MoneyPolicy, a native profit measurement or an automatic pause. With no paid linkage, give the useful conditional limit from supplied m/t and identify the missing current comparison.

## Budget proposal under business constraints

Before suggesting a bidding/strategy change, inspect the exact current captured strategy and its selected goal, finite period/budget and readiness from Configuration/catalog. A mature business-linked event and enough compatible observation can support a bounded strategy hypothesis; a technical click/event, sparse or immature outcomes, or unknown paid linkage cannot establish a business CPA target. Do not invent a universal event-count threshold, learning period or provider capability. The schema/catalog decides whether the selected change can be prepared; a missing supported form leaves a ready economic assignment.

For manual/traffic-oriented bidding, explain which observed traffic price/coverage and economic limit constrain the proposed test. For a goal-oriented strategy, explain the actual event/goal meaning, lag, observed stability and business limit; a low historical technical CPA is insufficient. Average `S/N` describes the past cohort. Marginal cost `(additional spend)/(additional paid sales)` needs an actual compatible increment/design; absent that evidence, future points are assumptions, not an optimization optimum. Start a chosen increase as a bounded feasible proposal with an agreed review condition and capacity guardrail; never infer the future sales count from the old average.

Read already saved budget/capacity restrictions and their applicability. Obtain only missing total B, intended period and currency/VAT basis, material lower/upper allocations, capacity restrictions and desired reserve. Distinguish a business minimum from observed past spend: history does not require repeating that spend. Keep unknown limits visible rather than inventing percentages, inventory or a reserve preference.

State the allocation rule and its status. For example, propose retaining the user's minima and reserve and directing discretionary budget to the service supported by the current contribution comparison, within its explicit ceiling. The rule is a proposal until actually chosen; a historical ranking does not itself establish an optimal split or marginal returns. If viability, capacity or the rule is unresolved, retain the corresponding amount as unallocated and name the one useful choice/data check. Do not force the entire budget to be spent.

Check feasibility before presenting a finished distribution: minima plus chosen reserve must fit B, each allocation must satisfy actual min/max and capacity, and allocations plus reserve/unallocated remainder must equal B. If constraints contradict, show the shortfall and which constraint needs reconsideration; do not silently weaken a minimum, create a negative reserve or fabricate approval. Show the amount per service, retained reserve/remainder, the rationale, unknown future performance and one next step. An economic allocation is a finite-period proposal; neither funding nor a provider-enforced cap has been established.

**Hypothetical continuation, not a live plan.** The user supplies a next-period project budget of 80 thousand RUB on the same VAT-excluded basis, reserve10, minima A30/B20, and ceiling B40 from its available capacity. The proposed rule is to direct the remaining discretionary20 to B, within that ceiling, while retaining minima/reserve. Present A30/B40/reserve10, total80, with the rule marked proposed or selected according to the actual chat choice. Explain why B cannot receive all80 and why the older CPA does not forecast new sales. The next step is to verify the current capacity/inputs and prepare the supported advertising change if the user chooses it. If the B ceiling is unknown, do not assume40: show the minima/reserve and the discretionary20 awaiting that check.

Use the existing [current action path](current-actions.md) for subsequent Ads preparation/application. Pass the saved economic grounds and actual chosen amounts/period, not a fabricated operation plan. The catalog, native shape/current versions and finite money guards remain authoritative. VAT-excluded project amounts do not directly become gross NativeMoneyPolicy; verified VAT, funding, overrun and provider semantics are separate. Unsupported preparation leaves a useful saved economic assignment and specific prerequisite. Track proposed economic allocation, prepared exact plan, applied/unknown state and later observation separately. No new effect is authorized merely by this handoff.

## Revisit the choice after payments, refunds or changed costs

At an ordinary weekly return or month-end, read the exact economic card and budget proposal, any actual selected plan/Application and only relevant changed evidence. Reuse unchanged inputs instead of asking for the whole brief. Establish whether the business correction concerns the same cohort and a later cutoff, a changed cost definition or a new comparable mature window. A late refund is not another sale batch to add. Carry source dates/versions, distinguish attributed updates from owner facts and retain the old result.

Show old → revised C, S, N and the resulting m/P/a/h, allocation grounds and remaining unknowns. For the same cohort, Δ(C−S)=ΔC−ΔS is a descriptive arithmetic change. If the business supplies compatible net-revenue/cost/refund detail, explain that breakdown without double-deducting refunds already included in net revenue. Without that detail, report only the known C/spend changes; do not identify price, returns or a proposed creative change as the cause. A selected t changes only from an actual business choice, not to make the result look acceptable.

**Hypothetical return:** late refunds revise B's C from80 to50 thousand RUB, with N10, S40 and t2 unchanged. Now m5/P3/a4/h−1 and C−S10. The earlier discretionary allocation to B has lost its stated grounds; propose reconsidering it or examining the changed contribution. Save a new revision with the exact predecessor and changed grounds. Do not automatically change/rollback the actual campaign or claim the revised contribution came from the proposed budget. If the incoming update lacks cohort/cutoff or refund inclusion, retain the old historical result, mark its current applicability uncertain and ask only for that missing distinction.

Regularity does not create a schedule: daily turns can inspect spend/known changes, weekly turns payments/refunds and conditions, monthly turns comparable closure and next budget. Mature source facts, human business updates and a causal conclusion remain separate. Use [Experiments](experiments-workflow.md) for a subsequent supported check of the chosen advertising change; a contribution calculation is not a supported native profit outcome.
