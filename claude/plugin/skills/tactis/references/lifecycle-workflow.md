# Lifecycle: next purchase, winback and repeat-sale feedback

Use for «Кому предложить следующую покупку?», «Как вернуть неактивных покупателей?» or «Увеличь повторные продажи, сохранив экономику». Finish purchase moment → group rule → offer and complete materials → manual application checks → matched repeat-payment observation → next decision. You reason and author in the external chat. When the installed tools expose the connected CRM/Lifecycle operations below, backend Sales reads purchases and Lifecycle stores a frozen group privately; the model receives aggregates only. Channel consent, suppression and delivery still need the client's existing system. This procedure never returns recipients or sends messages. Assisted reports remain an explicit fallback when the automatic source is unavailable.

## Resume the business and choose the case

Follow [durable continuation](durable-continuation.md) for authorized project/account, brief, confirmed context and relevant exact Work. Use [foundation reads](foundation-reads.md) only for needed owner evidence. Keep already supplied product, cycle, terms, channel, choices and source limitations; do not repeat a filled brief or demand a technical table upfront.

Read known product/offer/destination, purchase cycle and selected date, available client report, existing contact channel/resource and frequency limit. A landing read through `get_landing_manifest` / `get_landing_page` can support copy; it does not prove stock, last purchase or contact permission. Ask one missing question that changes the result. Use an existing small report or a brief aggregate answer from its authorized operator; give the operator a concrete filter/check when a value is absent. Do not request raw customer/order exports, names, emails or customer IDs.

`get_crm_publications` is optional evidence for the exact project/account/period and confirmed mapping. Its lead/status/campaign sales totals cannot recover customer identity, paid purchase history, next-purchase timing, inactive cohorts or eligibility. A mapped sale does not by itself prove payment. Quality/paid evidence from Sales remains limited to its stated source/coverage; a business-supplied cycle is reported, not measured by Tactis.

## Connected automatic cohort and outcome

Use the actual exposed Sales CRM status/capture/job/publication schema. Human setup supplies one encrypted read-only Bitrix24 webhook, selected funnel and explicit `buyer_mode=company|contact`; never request its secret or personal CRM export in chat. Native full-paid semantics are `crm_paid_deal_v1`, **CRM-reported gross paid**, not bank/fiscal settlement, net paid or WON. Company counts are business units, not people/recipients; keep the chosen mode visible. A funnel is not a product category. No any-paid fallback, custom-field editor or Lifecycle connector setup is part of this release.

Launch the existing source capture with `scope=all_visible_category` and continue through its durable job and exact completed publication ID. It reads at most 50 visible deals, one provider request per SourcesWorker advance, with shared budgets. It does not block the chat waiting for a synchronous CRM scan. Do not substitute `creation_window`, CRM totals or an independent manual table for complete history. Read partial/gaps before claiming a full count; a next page, missing buyer/payment or revoked source is not zero.

Prepare the selected exact material and agreed observation window first. Call `capture_lifecycle_cohort` with project, actual `source_publication_id`, new command key, `rule={scenario,min_age_days,max_age_days,timezone}`, exact bounded `material`, and actual aware `mature_at`. Age is calendar days `[min,max)` by the latest full-paid purchase in the entire visible history. Backend fixes `as_of` to source capture finish, not today's date or a supplied backdate. Immutable `id`/`fingerprint` and source ID identify the frozen group and exact material. `purchase_rule_count`/`original_denominator` are exact only for complete compatible all-visible scope; partial has observed counts/gaps, with exact counts null. Refund/channel/opt-out suppression is not proved: `ready_to_send` and channel/net counts remain unknown. Describe the output as a purchase-rule group, never eligible recipients.

On return use `get_lifecycle_cohort(project_id,publication_id)`; reuse exact rule/material/as_of rather than recalculating membership or asking for the brief again. For repeat purchases, run another existing Sales source capture, continue to its publication, then call `capture_lifecycle_outcome` with original cohort ID, later source ID and new command key; read back through `get_lifecycle_outcome`. Numerator includes only original buyers with a new qualifying order in `(cohort.as_of,later_source.finish]`; old orders paid later, newcomers and duplicate purchases by the same buyer do not inflate repeat buyers. Original denominator never changes. Partial source, identity/mapping drift or corrections produce null rate plus a gap; inaccessible source/custody may instead deny the operation. Zero denominator gives null; complete zero repeat buyers can give zero share. Maturity uses saved window and observed source end, never advancing wall clock. Gross observation proves neither delivery nor causal uplift/profit.

Save/read back Work with actual publication IDs and aggregate limitations; use `lifecycle_cohort`/`lifecycle_outcome` typed refs only when the installed schema exposes them and actual scoped get/fingerprint proof succeeds. Otherwise keep real locators as prose. Private buyer/order keys, webhook, names and addresses never enter Work or model context. Changed rule/material creates a new cohort publication with predecessor context; old cohort/outcome is never edited.

New cohort/outcome creation uses existing new-work access/usage admission. If it expires, retain the exact completed publication or useful draft and actual denied status; do not claim a new publication was saved. Exact get and same-command/input replay remain available while current read rights hold. No renewed provider-write grant is needed for this local admission check. Common API/MCP/plugin/skill/save-return semantics apply across Codex, ZCode and Claude; actual installed capability and final host acceptance are coordinator-owned.

Choose the useful case rather than mechanically producing both:

| Case | Concrete rule and useful material | Meaning of the interval |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Next purchase** | Relevant last paid, non-returned category purchase in a specified replenishment window; one reason to buy and one full contact | Based on the known cycle, or an explicitly proposed trial window; not a predicted individual purchase date |
| **Winback** | Relevant last purchase beyond the agreed usual cycle; distinct return offer, first contact and at most one conditional follow-up | A trial inactivity definition for this category, not proven churn. Lack of activity alone does not reveal why the buyer stopped |

If both are requested, use non-overlapping windows or explicit precedence/suppression in the client's system. Do not double-count buyers or contact the same person through two scenarios. Other categories and mixed baskets may overlap even when age windows differ; ask for the relevant existing aggregate overlap check, not identities. A distribution among customers who already reordered cannot establish the optimal interval for all customers still awaiting a repeat.

## Build the rule, offer and contact sequence

For each scenario name category/scope, last-purchase definition, date/timezone, paid/refund treatment and window. The automatic rule already uses the last qualifying purchase including fresh purchases; its group is privately frozen. Open orders, unresolved complaint/refund, channel permission, opt-outs and recent contacts still require the client's fresh checks. Unsupported/unknown exclusions leave contact eligibility unverified. In assisted fallback the client's system must also link and freeze the group; do not turn missing history into an empty segment.

Give the exact offer and full subject/body/destination for each intended contact. Use available products and supported terms; invent no discounts, prices, guarantees, personal history, urgency or uplift. A proposed economic incentive is not a confirmed offer. Preserve selected copy verbatim. If the destination or material claim is unresolved, provide the useful text with that precise missing item and label it draft.

Default to one contact. A second winback contact needs a real chosen interval, a functioning existing channel/team, permitted frequency and appropriate user choice. State fresh suppression before it: remove new purchasers, opt-outs, complaints and anyone reaching the contact limit. If fresh suppression is unavailable, do not recommend the follow-up as ready. A reply-based offer requires a team that actually answers. Stop on the stated conditions; opening/clicking is not a paid purchase. No chosen date means no fabricated date or automatic schedule. Already authorized exact scope does not require repeated consent; a new recipient/channel/material action is not authorized merely by saved Work.

Present **rule → reason/offer → full messages → manual checker/channel → stop and state → next check**. The manual package includes selection version/date, eligibility/suppression, exact texts/destinations, performer, timing if actually chosen and observation condition. Hand it to the user in this chat. An internal handoff is not emailing the client, posting to a CRM, assigning a new external person or executing a campaign. Apply the [selection and feedback procedure](lifecycle-feedback.md) before claiming usable counts or interpreting outcomes.

## Conditional normal example: coffee

Illustrative only. Suppose the owner reports a roughly monthly cycle, available coffee, a shop employee with an existing permitted email channel and functioning replies. The URLs below are synthetic; replace them with the actual known destination before using the material.

**R1 next purchase:** last paid, non-returned coffee purchase 28–42 days before the chosen date; no later order. Offer: replenish a familiar sort without a discount. Subject: «Пора пополнить запас кофе?» Body: «Пора пополнить запас кофе? Выбери привычный сорт: https://coffee.example.invalid/coffee».

**R2 winback:** last relevant purchase 90–180 days before the same date, no later order. Offer: return to a familiar range without an invented discount. Subject: «Вернуться к любимому кофе». Body: «Хочется снова выбрать кофе для дома? Посмотри знакомые сорта и выбери тот, который нравится: https://coffee.example.invalid/coffee». Neither message asserts an individual's prior order.

Both rules include the exclusions above and require real checks in the shop system. These age windows are hypotheses for this illustrative category; do not reuse them as universal defaults. If a second contact is actually chosen and all fresh checks pass, its subject is «Помочь выбрать кофе?», body «Не знаешь, какой сорт выбрать? Ответь на это письмо: подскажем кофе по твоим предпочтениям». Without the reply resource, real timing or suppression, retain only the first contact. The employee performs any authorized contact separately. All selected texts, conditions and unresolved items are saved, not just a recommendation label.

## Missing data still yields useful work

With only legacy CRM totals or no usable connected source, explain that recency/group size remain unknown; do not ask for a manual table when a supported automatic capture is available. If the repeatable product is known, prepare a concrete first message and the selection check; ask only the missing cycle or existing channel question. If the cycle is absent, do not invent a churn threshold. If the channel or destination is absent, call the result a draft, not ready to send. If the product is unknown, use available business evidence then save the precise remaining product question rather than empty scenario fields.

An incomplete manual report yields an exact corrected rule and one small report request under [feedback](lifecycle-feedback.md). An unmatchable outcome yields useful application status/next check but no repeat-payment rate. Distinguish prepared material, client-reported selection, client-reported application and observed matched payments. Without their prerequisites, selection/application/outcome are not completed. Do not create a CRM reader, Direct audience or fake Application to conceal the boundary.

## Save the exact scenarios and resume

Use the actual `record_work_session` schema, normally `kind=assessment`. Keep one scenario per bounded `summary` (maximum 4000 characters): source/status, case and local label, exact rule/date, offer and all selected texts, channel/checker, eligibility/stop, observed or reported state, limitations and next result. Two short contacts and a small count summary fit a compact scenario; preserve complete text and material limitations. A weekly assessment may point to both exact scenario Work IDs. Local R1/R2 labels have meaning only with their admitted Work IDs; they are not global cohort IDs. Preserve prior Work/source/campaign locators only when actually supplied/read. Do not invent saved IDs.

Private CRM IDs never belong in Work. A client's reported group/report locator, user statements and prior Work IDs are attributed prose locators. Backend Lifecycle IDs can use supported `lifecycle_cohort`/`lifecycle_outcome` owner refs after scoped get/fingerprint proof; they are not `facts_ids`. Do not manufacture A1/`ExternalAssessment`, context versions, goal or mature measurement. Follow durable continuation for head/snapshot/replay/readback; ordinary Work is valid without real head prerequisites/revisit conditions. Saving needs no separate permission. Verify exact saved Work before claiming persistence; denied or ambiguous save retains its actual status.

**Intermediate task safety:** when a source task is not finished, use task-linked Work only if the current schema explicitly supports the Platform keep_pending behavior, with its exact advertised field. Otherwise omit `task_id`, keep the real source task locator and remaining result in the summary/next_step, and save ordinary Work. Do not claim the task completed or invent a keep_pending parameter. The frozen source closes a task when `task_id` is supplied; a prepared package or cross-product intermediate is not completion.

On return, read the full head plus exact scenario/detail, or Work collection/detail when the scenario is outside the head. Recover unchanged exact copy rather than regenerate it, check changed cycle/offer/channel/group freshness and continue the specific remaining step. Save a new Work with the actual predecessor locator and changed condition. A closed host does not reason, check fresh suppression or send messages; a date does not create a schedule.
