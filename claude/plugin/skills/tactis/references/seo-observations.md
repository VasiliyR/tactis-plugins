Retain that meaning, source/configuration provenance, observed_at, query/window, rows, sampling, coverage and publication fingerprint. Visits are not Webmaster clicks, any page view, index presence or causal SEO effect. Goal visits are technical converted visits until their business meaning is established, not qualified leads or sales.

Missing URL/device rows are not zero. Reading the saved receipt does not refresh its date or mature it. To acquire newer facts, perform a new scoped capture with a new command key, retaining the old receipt as history.

## Fallback: interpret an existing supplied report

Ask for a usable existing report for the selected one to three pages, not a new large spreadsheet, hand-computed indicators or JSON. Read a legible supplied report/text/snapshot using actual host capabilities. If a binary document cannot be read, request the relevant text portion with its report heading/filters. Never create or upload customer records to unblock this route. Retrieve known site, page, material, publication statement and previous observations yourself; a locator in prose is not authenticated provider evidence.

Extract a compact table with the original values and their provenance: site/counter or report locator, exact URL, metric/unit, period and report date, engine/source filters, attribution when relevant and available coverage/sampling limitations. For comparison use at most two completed windows. Clarify only missing scope that changes the decision. If a source/date/filter cannot be established, label it unknown and limit the claim; a general “visits” row cannot become organic visits. Retain raw relevant rows alongside authored calculations, not just the final interpretation.

Keep these separate:

- Wordstat frequency is overlapping query demand for its exact phrase, region, devices and trailing window. It is not the site's ranking, clicks or visits.
- Webmaster impressions/clicks/CTR/average positions are source-reported search visibility. Its page/query totals can include organic results and dynamic placements; mixed data stays mixed unless the supplied evidence actually separates it. Do not subtract incompatible Direct data to infer organic counts. Position is an average for the declared scope, not an exact rank at a chosen moment.
- Metrika “Search engines” reports can provide search-source visits with page-of-entry scope and attribution. Inspect the actual source/filter; visits are not Webmaster clicks. Tracking/tagging/attribution gaps remain limitations.
- Query rows can be suppressed or absent; missing rows are not zero. A technical goal is not a qualified lead or sale without its business meaning.

These source meanings are documented in [Webmaster page statistics](https://yandex.ru/support/webmaster/ru/service/urls), [query statistics](https://yandex.ru/support/webmaster/ru/service/statistics), [Metrika search engines](https://yandex.ru/support/metrica/ru/sources/search-engines.html) and [search-query limitations](https://yandex.ru/support/metrica/ru/sources/search-queries.html) (reviewed 2026-10-09). Provided data remains attributed, not a new typed owner publication. Do not infer full-site coverage from a few selected pages.

## Compare only what can be compared

Check the same site/counter, exact page identity or explicitly documented redirects, metric units, source/engine filters, attribution, device/region selection and report limitations across completed windows. Window lengths/calendar composition, seasonality, other interventions, tracking changes and indexing delays affect interpretation. Publication and capture dates do not prove when indexing occurred. Never invent an indexing date or universal “SEO effect after a week”. Record source observation date separately from the date of this review.

Show original values and a supported difference. Percentage change from a zero denominator is undefined; confirmed zero is distinct from absent/unavailable. Compute CTR only from compatible clicks/impressions of the same source/scope, not visits; do not average row percentages into a site total. Different or unknown filters permit separate historical statements, not a growth percentage. Sparse goal counts do not establish a winner. All before/after comparisons here are observations, not causal effect of the material.

Return **one page decision**: retain the page/current material, prepare a specific improvement, choose another grounded page task, or wait for one necessary source condition. Explain the supporting evidence and uncertainty. A lack of mature data need not block an already grounded editorial correction. If no correction is justified, do not write new content simply because a follow-up happened. Attach the next small material when supported, or a concrete editor/data task.
