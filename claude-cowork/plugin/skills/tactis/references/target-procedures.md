# Target-native marketing procedures

Use the current MCP schemas and selected task scope. These procedures select evidence and reasoning; they add no payload fields. Apply [entry](session-entry.md) only for a missing scope/goal decision. Known original locators go directly to [saved results](saved-results.md); a continuation read is needed only for relevant missing saved meaning. Follow immutable catalog/pages for the scope of this decision, not an account-wide audit by default.

For a concrete input → output recipe, use [evidence-to-decision recipes](profiled-reasoning.md). [Shared invariants](invariants.md) owns consent, typed evidence, idempotent recovery, CAS and pending tasks; short reminders remain beside execution/save. New client/event checks go directly to [onboarding](onboarding.md), supported SEARCH stages to [launch](campaign-launch.md), and original-question examples to [three traces](workflow-traces.md).

For the connected Ads cycle, enter [Ads](ads-workflow.md) at diagnosis, existing improvement, SEARCH preparation or return to a saved result. Use only the relevant procedure below for its evidence or exact operation; do not restart the client's filled brief. For a check of the chosen change, continue through [Experiments](experiments-workflow.md) with the exact material and existing user choice.

When several independent immutable evidence reads are needed, use `read_evidence_bundle` for up to eight supported reads in the selected project. It returns the original owner data with per-item status; partial or oversize items require granular drill-down. A duplicate points to its original item, including that item's unavailable state. Do not infer completeness from the bundle's existence or combine incompatible windows.

## Business, site and goals

Read the selected account's available ads and scoped landing artifact through `get_landing_manifest` / `get_landing_page`. Separate extracted claims from your business hypothesis and from human-confirmed memory. A fetched single document does not establish a whole site or unseen JavaScript content. Ask only about ambiguity material to the current decision. For goal choice, inspect the exact counter/goal definition, selected binding, observed delivery and business meaning; do not silently replace a selected goal or equate an event with a qualified lead. Apply directly stated business fields through `update_business_profile` without repeating the choice. Keep inferred proposals uncertain until the material factual choice is resolved; preserve results when the shared [save stage](durable-continuation.md#save-the-result-and-next-step) applies.

## Account diagnosis and economics

Use `get_analysis_manifest`, the complete campaign catalog and exact campaign references; use dossier families for deeper questions. Coverage and comparable windows precede interpretation. Separate traffic volume, CPC, conversion rate, event CPA and CRM business outcomes. Compare segment composition before blaming within-segment performance. Consider seasonality, tracking changes, attribution, learning, lag and overlapping interventions. Rank a small number of useful next decisions by evidence, impact, uncertainty and reversibility; a no-action with a meaningful revisit is valid.

## Search relevance, keywords and negatives

Inspect exact query/keyword/match operators, campaign/ad/landing context, outcomes and source coverage. Decide whether the intent fits the offer and business, not whether it contains a disfavored word. Brand, competitor, informational and low-volume queries can be useful. For a new semantic set, use acquired demand evidence only when its provider access, region, query form and receipt are known; otherwise distinguish ideas from measured frequency. Never sum overlapping phrase counts into unique audience or guaranteed clicks.

Group intent and write keyword suggestions externally. Before proposing negatives, compare every negative in the declared set against useful commercial intents, retain unresolved cases and describe possible demand loss. A source-procedure positional verdict does not authorize deletion. Build the exact supported add/remove payload from the current catalog, fresh native scope and existing set; do not replace a whole set from a partial page.

## Creative and landing consistency

Ground every factual promise in fetched evidence or confirmed business context. Preserve actual geo, price, availability and guarantee conditions. Explain the creative hypothesis and what remains constant in the proposed comparison. Obtain the supported provider fields and limits from `get_operations_catalog`; author within that schema rather than legacy source examples. A weak CTR alone does not establish poor business performance, and an unequal-exposure creative comparison is not a randomized winner.

## Creation and change proposals

For URL-to-campaign work, establish offer, audience/intents, geography, landing evidence, business goal and explicit financial bounds. Propose useful draft material before every unknown is resolved; keep missing launch conditions visible. Current supported creation begins PAUSED. Build exact operations from the actual catalog, evidence references, confirmed goal/memory versions and supported measurement intent. Reuse the user's existing choice for the exact action and scope; otherwise obtain it in chat before the external change. Moderation and paid launch are separate choices. Execute only through the supported exact-scoped tool, then verify its Application and provider readback. Never promote a preview, forecast or chat choice to an executed effect.

## Experiments and follow-up

Follow the [Experiments cycle](experiments-workflow.md) to choose a hypothesis/design, retain complete material and the original success condition/limits, read actual application and measurement, then decide and reuse the result. Resume the existing card at its current stage rather than preparing a new check each time. An ordinary check does not require a randomized test.

Existing native measurements are source-reported pre/post and retain their sufficiency/causality limits. For two known HTTPS pages, [the first-party routing link](experiments-workflow.md#first-party-routing-link) provides actual finite browser-cookie allocation and an assignment ledger through the existing Experiments owner; it changes neither site nor campaign and has no event join yet. Existing source refs are optional, not a requirement to prepare documents first. For a prospective external unique-user controlled design follow [the assisted recipe](assisted-experiment.md): `prepare_assisted_experiment` → mature actual external evidence through `record_assisted_experiment_outcome`; the full successful receipt is sufficient, exact reads restore missing or later state. Existing disjoint campaign arms, real unique-user equal allocation, sample/horizon/lag/guardrails and one final look are concrete prerequisites. External attestations/hashes are not independently verified provider proof; a conditional estimate neither launches campaigns nor proves causal business uplift.

## Work, proactivity and presentation

When the shared [save stage](durable-continuation.md#save-the-result-and-next-step) applies, record the analysis/decision and exact next check in Work using supported reference types. Use `continuation.mode="delta"` on the expected head and retain the committed save receipt; the owner carries unchanged active decisions/questions and original evidence. [Proactive delivery](proactive-delivery.md) names the separate policy/claim/state/ack tools: claim only under the existing host-bound policy; ack the exact current lease only after its completed task result and committed saved Work receipt. Intermediate Work stays keep_pending and is not acked. Without a verified host scheduler, tasks persist for the next external turn; no hidden model runs while the host is unavailable.

Present analysis, comparisons and proposals in chat using the bundled visual-analysis skill. Reports are optional explicit deliverables. Historical evidence keeps its original method/version, even after an updated skill reinterprets it in a new Work session.
