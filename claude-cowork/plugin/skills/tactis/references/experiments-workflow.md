# Choose, check and reuse an advertising hypothesis

Use when the user asks what to check first, wants the material for a check, returns to its action or observations, or wants to reuse an earlier conclusion. Complete the available next result: a grounded choice and material, an actual state with a return condition, or a saved decision with applicability. Read the actual tool schemas. Use [foundation reads](foundation-reads.md) for relevant facts, [current actions](current-actions.md) for supported Ads plans and [durable continuation](durable-continuation.md) for saving and returning. This procedure adds no execution authority or measurement tool.

## Choose a useful hypothesis and design

Before selecting another hypothesis, [reuse prior learning](prior-learning.md). Restore the original criterion and material, assess applicability and keep negative/inconclusive results. Save the chosen hypothesis and its later result in `learning` alongside exact owner evidence; a new test does not erase the earlier finding.

Read the existing project/account context and exact relevant Work/material before asking the user to fill anything in. When there are competing ideas, present up to three grounded hypotheses with their evidence, expected usefulness, available material/action, missing inputs and risk to useful demand. This is a short chat shortlist, not a backend registry or numeric priority model. Distinguish known facts from the proposed mechanism. Select one against the user's actual objective and constraints; retain the reason and the conditions under which deferred alternatives become useful. If the user already selected one, keep that choice and evaluate feasibility without forcing another selection.

For example, when repair queries consume spend for a furniture seller, compare relevant negative phrases with the alternative of rewriting ad text. Prefer the cleanup only if the offer, query evidence, full existing negative set and supported action make it usable; retain the text idea with its material prerequisite. Do not infer a causal ad-level winner from campaign totals. If both ideas lack suitable evidence, save the bounded shortlist and one concrete data question rather than manufacture a preferred test.

Choose the design from the question and available evidence:

| Design | Usable result and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Technical verification | Exact material/configuration/readback verifies what changed; it cannot decide business benefit. |
| Native observational comparison | Comparable baseline and post-window for the actual campaign scope; inspect goal, units, coverage, lag and concurrent changes. Source-reported pre/post is noncausal. |
| Prospective controlled assisted | Only with the actual external allocation and binary-user prerequisites; follow the existing assisted branch below. No universal A/B intake or automatic allocation. |
| Defer judgement / request data | Preserve useful material and the exact missing prerequisite or meaningful traffic condition; no invented result or statistical power. |

For the first supported change, inspect `negative_keywords` in the current catalog. Prepare the exact proposed full replacement set for the selected campaign/group, showing retained phrases, additions/removals and the risk of excluding commercial intent. Do not present an unsupported creative or page writer as available. Technical verification and business observation may both be useful, with separate conclusions.

## Prepare a useful card

Resume the authorized project/account and relevant Work first. Read known business constraints, the exact campaign evidence and a completed baseline window yourself. Ask only about a material unknown: the intended change, meaning of the event, acceptable spend or deterioration, or a missing source/lag. Do not present allocation, hashes, alpha or power as an intake questionnaire.

Choose one change with a grounded hypothesis. Inspect the actual operation catalog before describing it as supported. For negatives, check the offer and useful commercial intents; preserve the complete existing set under replacement semantics. Partial query evidence can support a bounded suggestion, not a claim that all unsuitable traffic was found. No provider write or fresh writable native scope is required merely to save a card. An unsupported shape remains a draft with its specific prerequisite.

Present a short, complete card in chat and retain the same material in Work:

- **Hypothesis and change:** one object and exact proposed material, what remains constant, and the grounds for expecting benefit. A description is not a frozen executable plan.
- **Baseline:** the real metric, value when known, completed dates, scope, source and units. Keep the source event separate from qualified leads/sales; retain coverage, goal and attribution limits. Never invent a publication or turn missing into zero.
- **Observation:** primary metric, relevant guardrail metrics, proposed future window and conversion lag with their grounds. Before a frozen intent exists, dates are conditional on actual preparation/application; the next check is the action state/date, not a promised result date. An unknown lag stays unknown. Seven days is an illustrative calendar, not proof of sufficient data.
- **Limits and decision:** the confirmed spend constraint or the explicit unknown, tolerated deterioration when established, and what would justify keeping, reconsidering or declining to judge the change. A proposed spending bound is not an enforced cap or a budget change. Check coverage, comparable goal/source/units, exposure and concurrent changes before interpreting differences. Lower spend alone cannot establish success when needed business guardrails are unknown.
- **Return:** one useful next step and its real condition; use an aware due date only when supported by actual facts. No invented deadline, reminder or background reasoning.

Illustrative case only, not client evidence or a ready tool payload: a furniture shop wants to exclude repair queries in one search campaign. If the offer and read query evidence support that choice, the card proposes the exact relevant negatives while preserving the previous list. Read actual campaign spend in RUB excluding VAT for the baseline. Propose a comparable seven-day observation window, with lag taken from available goal/source evidence; monitor the exact application event and CPA only if their inputs are compatible. Keep the existing budget, naming its known constraint or uncertainty. After the actual mature window, review spend and preservation of the event alongside coverage and other changes. Do not claim causal savings or automatically apply/roll back the negatives. Example wording: «Предлагаю проверить исключение запросов про ремонт. Сначала сохраним условия оценки; после применения сверим фактическое окно замера. Снижение расходов без данных о заявках не назовём успехом.» Actual baseline figures, exact phrases and dates must come from this project's evidence.

If spend is available but the goal definition or observations are missing, save the useful partial card with its known baseline and exact proposed change. Name the one needed follow-up, such as checking the selected goal definition and compatible observations. CPA and preservation of applications remain unknown. With too little traffic, declining to judge the change by applications is a useful saved decision, with a meaningful reconsideration condition. Do not fabricate an experiment or force a statistical test to complete the card.

## Save the complete material

Use `record_work_session` with the supported `proposal` or `no_action` kind, bounded summary, limitations and next_step. Keep the full compact card and exact proposed material, not only its title or a reference to unsaved chat. Keep it within the actual schema limits; shorten surrounding prose, never silently discard essential phrases, baseline, constraints or decision conditions. If complete material cannot fit, explain the unsaved portion and retain an explicitly bounded card rather than claiming full persistence.

Cite only actual read publications in foundation_refs. Ordinary Work is valid without an A1 or continuation; do not invent an ExternalAssessment, head, context version or due date to save it. When continuation prerequisites exist, use the linked compact `continuation.mode="delta"` procedure, preserving existing decisions and questions. Unsupported conclusions retain uncertainty, limitations and a meaningful next check. A committed Work receipt confirms saving; exact reads serve history, conflicts, required material content or recovery. Work alone does not mean the current continuation head was updated. Save failures/ambiguous responses follow the linked unsaved/unconfirmed rules; saving analysis needs no extra consent.

An intermediate card, material or review uses `task_id` with `task_resolution="keep_pending"`. Complete the originating task through Work `task_resolution="complete"` only when its requested result is actually delivered; the committed receipt confirms that save. Read the exact saved result or current task status only when its content, a later status, conflict or recovery is needed.

## Return to facts, not the draft calendar

Read brief, the supported full continuation when available, and the exact relevant Work. Without a head, use existing Work selectors; an absent head does not mean absent history. Restore the card and perform one available step: resolve its material question, locate the actual application, or read its measurement.

When an exact Ads plan exists, read `get_operations_plan` and `get_operations_application` in its authorized scope. `OperationsProposal.measurement` becomes frozen `measurement_scope` inside that plan; a separate MeasurementIntent is registered at execution admission and linked through Application.measurement_id. A prepared plan or registered intent does not prove provider effect. There is no standalone prepare-native-measurement tool. Do not call execute merely to create a measurement, nor move frozen windows to fit a delayed action. Later execution uses the existing exact Ads lifecycle and the user's already applicable authorization.

For a supported executable plan, use the current Ads procedure to obtain the actual scope, configuration, catalog, versions and analysis basis, then propose the complete material and measurement settings. Native comparison currently supports spend_rub, conversions and cpa_rub; do not substitute qualified leads or sales. Check the actual schema/builder window limits (native days plus lag at most 60), comparable baseline and the returned frozen dates against the business deadline before action. Use `foundation_refs` with `kind="operation_plan"`, the actual plan ID, account ID and plan digest. If the whole replacement set exceeds Work limits, keep it complete in an exact-readable immutable plan and save the compact card plus that locator; without such storage, explicitly report incomplete persistence. Do not silently truncate phrases. Apply only under the exact applicable action authority. Ambiguous/lost execution responses follow common Ads recovery; no blind new command to obtain a measurement or receipt. Read an Application by its actual `application_id`; a plan locator does not become an Application locator.

Use the actual measurement_id with `get_native_measurement`. If the locator is absent, page `list_native_measurements` for this account and verify the exact plan/Application/campaign linkage; do not select an unrelated recent measurement. Read `get_native_outcome` only for the returned latest_outcome_id when present. Use the frozen baseline, period, lag and returned maturity_at instead of conditional dates from the card. Reading an existing outcome suffices here; forced observation or new collection is not required.

Explain awaiting effect, awaiting maturity, missing/unavailable or incompatible data, and observed outcomes according to owner evidence. Calendar maturity alone does not establish coverage or sufficiency. Read confounders, sufficiency and limitations even when a verdict says confirmed or no_effect. Native source-reported pre/post is observational with causal_claim=false; it is not randomized A/B, a statistical winner or proven business uplift. Save the limited interpretation, applicability and next step as `experiment_review`, using exact native_measurement/native_outcome foundation references where available, and retain its committed receipt; no routine Work readback is needed. New interpretation waits for an external agent turn; a closed host does not promise review or notification.

For a genuinely prospective controlled design with suitable external allocation and binary-user evidence, go directly to [the complete assisted recipe](assisted-experiment.md): preparation → exact readback → mature final evidence → readback/review. Its attestations/hashes remain external and unverified, and it grants no campaign launch or causal proof. It is optional, not a prerequisite for the ordinary check card.

## Decide against the original condition

Restore the exact original card and frozen plan before interpreting outcomes. Make a compact table: original condition/guardrail, actual fact and exact source/window, met/violated/unknown, implication. Keep the original wording and threshold; a new rule belongs to a new prospective check. If the original material is missing, label the review retrospective and preserve that limitation. Do not manufacture a preregistered criterion from the result.

Keep the owner's verdict unchanged alongside the specialist's limited decision. A confirmed operation or native verdict does not establish qualified leads, profit or causal uplift. Suitable coverage, compatible inputs and known guardrails may justify retaining the change as a bounded working choice; a violated meaningful guardrail may justify reconsideration; missing/confounded/insufficient evidence means defer business judgement with one useful next check. A recommendation to revert is material for the Ads lifecycle, not a performed rollback or new authority. Save `experiment_review` with the original Work locator, actual plan/Application and publication locators, unchanged criteria, decision, limitations, applicability and return condition. Use typed refs only where supported; Work and Application IDs remain ordinary exact locators.

## Reuse and the next weekly cycle

On return, recover relevant exact Work through the continuation or paged Work history, checking project/account linkage. Do not select the newest record indiscriminately or invent a semantic search/experiment registry. Read its original material and decision, then check what changed: offer, objective/goal, campaign/group, negative set, source/units, observation window, seasonality and concurrent changes. A historical observation stays historical; it supplies a constrained idea, not current execution permission or a prediction of benefit.

If applicability still holds, use the saved reason to avoid repeating the same failed or completed check and produce the next shortlist/card/material. If scope or evidence changed, retain the old conclusion in its original scope and prepare a revised hypothesis with its changed basis. If the old Work cannot be recovered, ask for the one locator/material needed and deliver what current evidence allows. Each weekly revisit should finish a useful decision or next material; at the monthly programme review pass selected decisions and unresolved conditions back to Growth/Reports. Recurrence does not imply a schedule exists; Automation handles a separately supported schedule, with actual host readiness.

## X5: programme material → check → Growth / Ads decision

Receive the selected Growth programme work, its local item label, exact saved material/Work locator, project/account, user choice, resources/deadline and constraints. Read the exact original records, preserving filled business brief, primary question and any Profit guardrail; ask only for a material missing input. A local label identifies an item within that Work, not a new global ID. Choose a supported first check from the programme; other product branches are optional.

For a negative-keyword change, carry exact phrases, campaign/group and the original criterion into the card and actual Ads plan, then return through the linked Application/measurement chain. Hand Growth the exact original programme/item locator, card/material locator, plan/Application when real, observed publication locators when real, criterion table, limited decision, applicability and next work/return condition. Hand Ads a precise retained or revised proposed set and the decision's scope; Ads owns supported preparation/action/readback. The saved decision enables the next step without repeating the brief or requesting the same already applicable consent.

For a Conversion/Creative page change (also applicable to X2), keep the exact selected copy/page version, original criterion, reported publication date/source and any actual public-version evidence. External CMS publication is an attributed or separately verified action; it does not create an Ads Application or native measurement. Record manual/source observations with their actual windows, coverage and provenance, separately from native outcomes; concurrent traffic/ad changes and missing attribution limit interpretation. Never attach an unrelated native campaign measurement to claim it measured that page change. Return a bounded editorial/observational decision and the specific source needed to decide business benefit.

Missing programme material gives a useful draft plus the exact missing locator/version, without fake plan or observation. In a new chat, reread the original programme, selected material, card and latest actual state, perform the available next step and save its decision with the original linkage. If still awaiting action/maturity, return that state and its factual condition; do not report the programme or task done merely because a Work was saved. Apply the intermediate task rule above.
