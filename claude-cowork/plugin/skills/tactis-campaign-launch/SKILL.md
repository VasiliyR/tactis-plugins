# Tactis — создание и запуск кампаний

Deliver the requested stage from the known offer and constraints. Use [entry](../tactis/references/session-entry.md) for missing scope and [choose a working goal](../tactis/references/session-entry.md#choose-a-working-goal) from the available evidence. Reuse selected copy, applicable goals and relevant [prior learning](../tactis/references/prior-learning.md).

Prepare [a coherent recommendation](../tactis/references/campaign-launch.md#recommend-the-campaign): purpose, audience/channel, geography, complete material, defensible budget/time proposal and measurement. Own these choices; ask only for a material business restriction that known context cannot resolve. Finish independent preparation while a required choice or access remains open.

For «создай кампанию для сайта», inspect the pages and relevant history, real queries, demand or event results that could change the offer, structure or strategy. Reuse suitable publications; reading every source is unnecessary. Choose the business event and obtain its supported measurement scope when conversion optimization needs it. [The strategy route](../tactis/references/campaign-launch.md#choose-a-supported-performance-strategy) distinguishes a finite click experiment from conversion/value optimization and supplies exact typed inputs.

For selected image Materials, include their actual Material IDs/hashes through `ads[].images` in the supported PAUSED Creator graph. Complete title/text alternatives are supported in the same graph. Use [image upload and exact ad attachment](../tactis/references/current-actions.md#upload-and-attach-selected-ad-images) for later changes to existing ads. Image creation/upload, attachment, moderation and paid delivery remain distinct proven stages within the same requested work.

For a goods catalog, use the supported [feed-driven ShoppingAd route](../tactis/references/current-actions.md#create-a-feed-driven-product-ad) after actual PAUSED campaign/group creation. Follow its retained feed, native processing and exact new ad identity through the same task; pending processing remains the original Application.

For a material draft, read [the material stage](../tactis/references/campaign-launch.md#construct-the-first-usable-material). Give intent groups or the supported NETWORK structure, complete copy, destination, applicable keywords/negatives and known settings. Ground promises in the offer or attributed page evidence. Missing measured demand leaves an estimate, not measured frequency.

For actual creation, include the complete prepared settings in that same `create_campaign`: campaign `negative_keywords`, `groups[].negative_keywords` for group exclusions/cross-negatives, and `schedule` for the selected seven-day local calendar. Use the shared schedule semantics and preserve exact phrases. Carry already applicable choices through the initial PAUSED graph and readback without another plan or a second client agreement merely to add these settings.

| Requested stage | Result to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Prepare material | Complete usable recommendation and copy, with unresolved conditions |
| Create a campaign | A supported non-serving graph with actual new IDs and per-object readback: native `SUSPENDED` or exact `OFF` + `DRAFT` |
| Moderate | The exact submitted objects and actual moderation/readiness state |
| Start paid delivery | The authorized finite launch, actual serving state and agreed observation condition |

When constructing or continuing a provider plan, read [campaign preparation and launch](../tactis/references/campaign-launch.md) for the exact capability, geography, financial and measurement prerequisites, and the relevant [current actions](../tactis/references/current-actions.md) section for proposal/execution/recovery. Creating a draft does not authorize spending. Moderation under an owner pause preserves it; submitting an `OFF` + `DRAFT` campaign can start delivery and requires the supported finite money plan and an applicable spending choice. Preserve current rights, exact scope and applicable user choices; resolve an unknown external outcome under the existing operation identity before another effect.

Continue through the stages already authorized while their prerequisites hold. Return the actual stage and useful material, or the exact remaining blocker. Queued, partial and unknown states remain visible. Provider eligibility, observed impressions, mature results and causal benefit are different claims.

Use [the save stage](../tactis/references/durable-continuation.md#save-the-result-and-next-step) for requested persistence or a selected result that needs continuation. Resume the existing material and Plan/Application rather than creating a second campaign.
