# Portfolio priorities and a grounded offer

These are two optional user-requested tasks over current capabilities. Choose the requested task, not both. Scope/authority/save rules belong to [invariants](invariants.md); [durable continuation](durable-continuation.md) owns Work save/recovery/return.

## Portfolio: one next step per authorized client

Use known selected projects. If selection is unknown, `list_projects` lists authorized projects; use active projects for current priorities and include archived history only when explicitly requested, following [session entry](session-recovery.md#archived-project). `get_project_brief` establishes the chosen project's state, client/account and recent Work locators. The tool is not a global performance dashboard. For each selected project separately, resume its relevant exact `get_work_session` and read only the source for its question through [foundation reads](foundation-reads.md). Do not carry an account/publication ID into another project or treat access to one client as access to all clients.

Produce an exception queue: **client/project · objective and source/window · known exception/unknown · priority reason · one next result · actual/proposed owner and return condition**. Use status/urgency relative to that project's agreed goal; no arbitrary cross-client efficiency ranking. A portfolio reader is unnecessary for a useful sequential review. Read the next project only if requested scope includes it; a bounded subset is labelled as such.

Compatible spend totals can be combined only when authorized scope, disjoint accounts, currency/VAT and completed dates agree. An unknown member leaves a known subtotal plus unknown remainder. Paid outcomes/CPA require identical definitions, maturity and proved denominators/linkage; unlike goals, currencies or cutoffs remain separate columns. Comparing rank within each client is often more useful than comparing incomparable CPA across clients. Never duplicate a shared account under two projects in a combined total.

Within one authorized project, an existing project-scope marketing matrix with exact Calltouch publications also returns a `calltouch_portfolio` source summary. The agent selects the existing source IDs and window; no new connection or mapping questionnaire is needed. `portfolio.observed_unique_calls` and `portfolio.observed_unique_forms` deduplicate only the exact Calltouch site ID, event family and native event ID across the selected sources/accounts. Calls and forms stay separate; identical numeric IDs at different sites stay separate. The summary shows duplicate observations, repeated events across accounts, calendar coverage and any conflicting IDs or unproven rows. Partial values are observed lower bounds excluding conflicting identities; unknown evidence never becomes zero. Follow the source references and preserve their actual observation times and selected population. A day unfinished at the source's observation remains partial when a later matrix is computed; elapsed time does not refresh the original capture.

These are unique provider events in the selected Calltouch population, not people, qualified leads, all business outcomes or cross-device conversions. ClientId, email, phone and name do not merge events. Keep spend in its original per-account matrix/source summaries with its evidence, currency and VAT; this deduplication does not allocate account costs to calls/forms or establish campaign attribution, CPL or CPA. Aggregated CRM/goal totals cannot be deduplicated this way without exact compatible event evidence.

Save each substantive client finding to that **client's** Work and retain its committed receipt; keep the portfolio queue in the current chat with those actual locators. Do not persist one client's private evidence in another client's Work. A user-requested consolidated artifact needs an actual permitted destination/host capability. Returning reads the chosen client's exact Work and current relevant state; it does not rerun every client's entire audit.

**Synthetic queue:** client A has verified zero delivery after a current captured pause, with date/intent still unknown → confirm intended delivery/state before changing anything. Client B has complete spend but unknown paid linkage → one Sales aggregate request. Client C has no new mature observation → keep its chosen experiment pending. These are useful different next steps, not a common “optimize all budgets” action.

## Offer: facts → hypothesis → usable first material

For Wordstat access, source failures and calendar history, follow
[the demand source contract](source-history.md#wordstat-demand).

Resume the known business, audience/offer Work and selected source. `get_project_brief` and confirmed context provide business facts; `get_landing_manifest`/`get_landing_page` provides exact public promises and source limits. An existing Audience Work adds its attributed needs; it is not verified demand or active targeting.

For an actual SEARCH demand question, use retained `get_demand_publication`, or supported `acquire_demand` with up to fifty known phrases and actual region/device inputs; every new request continues as one durable command with paged phrase results, following the source contract above. Demand is phrase request evidence with its date/coverage, not unique customers or purchases; overlapping phrases cannot be summed into a market size. `preview_demand_scenario` uses **explicit supplied assumptions** and one selected child publication, not a measured sales forecast. Competitor prices/reviews and market claims require a current supplied or actually available external source; no Tactis competitor/crawl reader is invented. If external browsing is needed and available, follow that host's source rules; do not expose client-private evidence in a search query.

Prepare one concise card:

- **Customer/task:** known customer facts versus proposed motive, with origin.
- **Offer:** exact product/service, destination, supported promise, terms/price only if sourced, one CTA.
- **Reason to believe:** claim → source/version/date → limit; unresolved claim gets a narrower supported wording or is omitted.
- **First material:** one full title/body/CTA or brief for the chosen format, via [Creative](creative-workflow.md). Unsupported images/publication stay separate production prerequisites.
- **Check:** one observable question and eligible source/window, with alternative explanation and business guardrail. Technical clicks/events do not prove paid demand.

**Synthetic example:** a supplied page says “ремонт кофемашин, диагностика перед согласованием работ”, with no timing/price guarantee. A useful draft is “Ремонт кофемашин / Сначала диагностика и согласование работ. Оставь заявку на проверку / [known destination]”. Motive “понять условия до ремонта” is a hypothesis; “за час”, “дешевле всех” and a discount have no grounds. Keep the actual format/provider limits when adapting, not this illustrative copy as a universal template.

Save the complete chosen card/material with its committed Work receipt with its real source refs and attributed choice, then prepare only the requested supported next action. Drafting an offer does not authorize provider/CMS publication, ad spend, contacting buyers or confirming new business memory. On return check changed terms/stock/landing and resume the exact material rather than rebuilding its intake.

## Source gaps: useful result now and what a further capability needs

Use this registry only when the current task actually needs a missing capability. It is not an implementation backlog or permission to build a new backend. Exact current catalog/schema wins over a historical limit; name the available partial result and one smallest prerequisite.

| Requested result | Current useful result | Missing data/action for the stronger claim |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sales stage conversion, response SLA or loss reason | Defined current CRM stage/payment groups and one Sales check | Matched stage-entry/exit and first-response timestamps, reasons, cutoff and open-cohort treatment; current totals/modification date are insufficient |
| Net paid/profit after refunds | CRM-marked gross paid plus conditional supplied contribution | Bank/refund/cost/tax evidence and compatible cohort/linkage; a successful stage or gross payment is insufficient |
| Activate an audience | Saved definition, observed source and Creative/Ads handoff | Actual supported target membership/apply operation, current authority and exact Plan; a persona alone has no membership |
| Publish/fix a page or prove its form works | Exact retained text/URL and ready correction/check | Supported CMS writer and public-version readback, or real form/test-event evidence; landing fetch/text does not prove submission |
| SEO indexing/crawl/positions | Bounded organic-entry/page observation and grounded text brief | Actual Webmaster/crawl/rank source, crawl coverage and dates; organic visits alone cannot prove indexing or rankings |
| Launch binary creative | Grounded exact text/brief and material version | Actual asset generation/format validation/upload provider contract and allowed application; a host image is not automatically an Ads asset |
| Send Lifecycle/measure causal repeat uplift | Frozen purchase-rule group, exact material and matched gross repeat observation where supported | Fresh channel consent/suppression/eligibility and delivery receipt; causal design and compatible costs for uplift/profit. Purchase counts never imply eligible recipients |

Record the gap and attainable next result in ordinary Work. Unknown is not zero and a missing writer is not a completed application. Reuse the stronger capability only when it is actually exposed and its source/authority gates are satisfied.
