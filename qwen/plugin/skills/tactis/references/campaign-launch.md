## Recommend the campaign

For a delegated campaign assignment, prepare a coherent recommendation: business purpose and metric, audience and channel, geography, complete offer/material and structure, budget and dates, conditional expected results and how to measure/review them. Explain the choices from the site, retained results, economics and constraints; do not return these as an empty form for the client. Reuse settled choices and include only dimensions relevant to the requested stage. For an explicitly requested channel, improve that solution rather than silently substituting another channel.

Use related campaign/ad history to identify reusable offers and known failed hypotheses; mine relevant search queries for customer language, intent and negatives. For a search hypothesis, acquire suitable measured demand when it can change the structure or test size and the source is available. Check relevant landing paths, event definitions and compatible Metrika results when they can change the destination, chosen event or strategy. Use CRM/payment evidence when it supports lead quality, value or the acquisition ceiling. Reuse fresh compatible publications and continue the same pending job. Missing optional history does not block the usable material, and authored intent does not become measured demand.

Match the strategy to the evidence for this campaign. A few whole-counter events do not establish enough relevant conversion history for conversion optimization or a campaign CPA. When history is sparse, prepare a bounded learning stage and explain the chosen strategy, observed event, cost limit and concrete condition for the next decision. Keep an assumed test size separate from an estimate supported by comparable results. State what makes the event useful for the business and which qualification facts remain unknown; do not make another questionnaire a prerequisite for a useful PAUSED draft.

Recommend geography from actual service coverage and relevant demand/results; ask only about an unknown commercial restriction. Recommend budget/time from defensible inputs within the known ceiling. Separate a conditional scenario from a forecast: show its cost/event assumptions, currency/VAT and qualification meaning. Without that basis, retain the unknown and give the concrete measurement/learning proposal. A proposed sum or scenario does not authorize spending. Follow [the shared recommendation and next-step rule](onboarding.md#own-the-recommendation-and-the-next-step): finish independent preparation, then ask the specific still-needed choice for this proposal, not “what geography and budget do you want?”

When service territory has not yet been confirmed, recommend a provisional geography from the offer, language, fulfilment and available demand, stating the assumption and asking about restrictions that could change it. An unknown territory field is not a reason to leave the recommendation empty; real targeting still waits for the required commercial confirmation and provider region proof.

## Construct the first usable material

Give the offer/URL, geography in ordinary language, intent groups, complete keywords/negatives, exact title/text and known format constraints. A useful draft needs no provider directory; unresolved provider IDs stay explicit.

Carry the full useful recommendation into the first `create_campaign` operation: campaign exclusions in `negative_keywords`, group exclusions and cross-negatives in each `groups[].negative_keywords`, and the selected campaign calendar in `schedule`. Preserve exact phrases; the compiler rejects phrases that need normalization. Do not leave these settings as prose or require a second plan or client agreement to attach already selected settings. The same PAUSED Plan/Application and readback verify the complete graph.

`schedule` uses the existing `campaign_schedule` settings: exactly seven `days` rows with `day=1..7` and 24 `coefficients=0..200` in steps of ten; zero stops that hour, with at least eight serving hours per week. Include `consider_working_weekends` and `suspend_on_holidays` when intended. Optional `schedule.timezone` selects the campaign's local IANA zone; omission retains the Creator default. Omit `schedule` when no calendar change is selected. Local serving hours do not shift the Moscow start/end, budget or report calendar. Read full campaign `time_targeting` for a varying week: a group's coarse `schedule=null` means there is no common daily hours list, not that serving is disabled.

When preparing the executable provider plan, resolve the actual geography name/code through `get_operations_catalog(include_regions=true, region_query=<selected place>, region_limit=...)`; reuse project/account and the needed bounded page. `region_reference.state=provider_receipt` identifies the actual directory; use its displayed `items` with `targetable=true`. Follow next_offset with the **same query and returned fingerprint** in `expected_region_fingerprint`; on a changed directory restart this choice rather than splice different snapshots. Default catalog metadata/a declared subset is not proof of the city's code. No region result or an unavailable provider leaves this plan prerequisite unresolved, not nationwide by default. Do not turn a remembered city integer into a Plan. The server separately checks selected codes before native preparation; a read receipt does not authorize targeting.

Missing measured demand need not block a grounded text draft; label estimated intent, not measured frequency. [Audience](audience-workflow.md) may contribute a saved preparation condition, but attach/suspend is unsupported and cannot be smuggled into this graph.

Use the retained source's exact `final_url` and `landing_source={artifact_id,fingerprint,evidence_refs}`. Span refs identify supporting text; legacy `evidence_ids` holds accepted retained object IDs, including this artifact. Preserve path/query/fragment and the selected URL; a guard failure does not permit silently choosing another destination. Binary assets and fields absent from this operation schema stay out.

## Resolve financial and measurement prerequisites before a Plan

For native Direct `create_campaign`, freeze the chosen configuration in `NativeMoneyPolicy`: exact start/end, gross period budget, provider ex-VAT spending limit, verified VAT rate and the user's authorized ceiling. Use `provider_semantics='direct_custom_period'` for one to ninety inclusive days. `possible_overrun_rub` defaults to zero: Tactis does not require an additional percentage reserve or an increased budget. Any explicitly chosen extra allowance must fit the authorized ceiling. Reuse the user's confirmed inputs; the future budget configuration does not authorize paid launch.

For the current native Creator inputs, prepare/read the exact `creator_account` proof, then read `get_operations_catalog` with the same explicit `project_id`, `account_id`, `operation="create_campaign"`, `include_money_reference=true` and `native_scope_proof_id`. Keep geography in a separate catalog read. `money_reference` supplies current normalized VAT and dictionary minima, with source/fingerprint and capture time. VAT is a fraction; provider limits are RUB before VAT. The documented RUB period minimum is max(300 RUB, 50 RUB × selected days); `MinimumDailyBudget` is a different setting and must not be multiplied by period duration. Use exact decimal arithmetic, preserve unknown fields and do not substitute fixture values. This reference is neither available funds nor write authority, and its fingerprint is not a stored publication or a Work foundation. See the [Direct period-budget rules](https://yandex.ru/support/direct/ru/strategies/period-budget).

Creating a PAUSED campaign does not spend money and may happen before its future start date. Unknown funding stays explicit `available_funding_rub=null`; a zero observed balance also does not block PAUSED creation. A new exact scoped Creator preview declares `funding_requirement=before_paid_delivery`, `draft_only=true`, `starts_money=false` and freezes the complete graph: campaign add → suspend before any ad add; no moderation or resume. Follow the applicable user choice and the normal exact Plan → execute → readback path. Paid delivery requires a separate selected plan with current provider funding evidence and its own launch checks. Previously saved `preparation_only=true` or `execution_allowed=false` plans retain their old non-executable state; prepare a new exact plan under the current contract instead of modifying their payload or digest.

Direct may refuse to suspend a newly created draft. The Creator then requires exact current `OFF` + `DRAFT` evidence before adding children and a complete non-serving graph readback. Report these actual native states: a non-serving draft is not an owner-suspended campaign. Moderation can remove the draft restriction, so its separate plan requires an actual owner pause; draft readback alone does not authorize or prove a safe moderation-to-delivery transition. Preserve a concrete unavailable transition instead of calling creation, moderation or launch complete.

The PAUSED Creator accepts the explicit optimization event in its typed `optimization` input. Conversion, CPA or revenue measurement requires that exact event and compatible source meaning. Refresh the relevant baseline and obtain actual accepted moderation before launch. A numerical launch target needs a grounded numerical basis; never insert zero to pass a schema. When a numerical success threshold is not justified yet, retain the business direction and a finite measurement stage with technical effect verification, the metric to observe and a stated return condition.

## Choose a supported performance strategy

Read the actual operation schema for `create_campaign` or `strategy`. Both use the existing finite money policy, exact source proof, dispatch and provider readback:

| Intent | Typed input | Provider result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Finite click experiment | `strategy="MAX_CLICKS"` | `WB_MAXIMUM_CLICKS`; it does not optimize conversions |
| Maximize the selected event without inventing CPA | `strategy="MAX_CONVERSIONS"`, `optimization={counter_id,goal_id}` | `WB_MAXIMUM_CONVERSION_RATE` with exact GoalId |
| Optimize to evidenced acquisition cost | `strategy="TARGET_CPA"`, exact `optimization`, positive `target_cpa_rub` | `AVERAGE_CPA`; money target converts to micro-RUB |
| Optimize a declared static event value | `strategy="TARGET_ROAS"`, exact `optimization` including positive `goal_value_rub`, `target_roas_ratio` as revenue/spend multiplier | `AVERAGE_CRR`; preview shows provider integer Crr and static `IsMetrikaSourceOfValue=NO` |

Use `get_yandex_measurement_catalog` with the existing Direct access, selected counter/campaign and needed goal pages to discover the actual site/event definition. `select_yandex_measurement_scope` retains an immutable read scope: counter-only for exploration, or the selected `goal_id` plus its returned `expected_goal_definition_fingerprint`. Its `source_binding_id` works with the existing standalone/project readers using `account_id=null`, including `get_metrika_goal_catalog` and `capture_metrika_counter_metrics`; it does not change the Direct default. Conversion writing separately consumes the current owner's proof for that exact counter/event and source generation. An event ID or a definition does not prove business meaning or real firing. Reuse the exact receipt without a repeated provider read; definition drift invalidates the frozen optimization proof. If an event is absent or unobserved, prepare that specific correction and continue campaign material. Do not substitute a button click for a lead or assign a fictional value.

All released strategies preserve the exact non-renewing custom-period budget and separate PAUSED creation from paid launch. Optional `bid_ceiling_rub` is supported for clicks, maximum conversions and target CPA. Static goal value is an attributed business assumption; use its known basis and explain it in the proposal. An existing `value_evidence_ref` is optional provenance, not a requirement to create memory or Work. Static value is never counted as observed revenue. Numerical `revenue_rub` uses actual compatible Metrika goal revenue in RUB. Cross-source ROAS is unavailable when spend/revenue money or attribution bases are incompatible. Provider optimization and observed business success remain separate claims.

Apply the shared [save stage](durable-continuation.md#save-the-result-and-next-step) when persistence is requested or a selected useful result needs continuation beyond this chat. An ordinary campaign draft, missing prerequisite or possible future check alone does not require Work. For the selected result, preserve actual material, existing Plan/digest/Application and native IDs, authority/coverage limits and one next step once; an existing material or execution receipt is not a reason to duplicate its contents in Work. Accepted execution retains its exact identity and recovery path. Complete task resolution waits for the requested result; pending creation, moderation or observation uses `keep_pending`. On a new host, read the same exact material and state instead of creating another campaign.

## Expansion, transfer and other campaign formats

For NETWORK, propose offer/audience groups and complete supported materials, with the expected intent and exclusions; do not copy SEARCH's keyword logic into placements. Retargeting uses [Audience](audience-workflow.md) for behaviour, windows, exclusions and actual consumers. Recommend the useful return stage/message yourself; an absent eligible list or targeting writer leaves a precise preparation package, not an assertion of attachment.

When selected image material is available, include its real immutable `{material_id,sha256}` through `ads[].images` in the same PAUSED Creator graph. Use the complete `copy.titles`/`copy.texts` shape for useful native alternatives; the existing single title/text shape also works. The graph uploads selected Materials and attaches only provider-confirmed hashes, with full text/image readback. [Exact image update](current-actions.md#upload-and-attach-selected-ad-images) serves later changes to existing ads. Omission requests no new image generation. Other binary formats and feed-driven campaigns retain their own capability limits.

For a product campaign, inspect the real assortment through [the feed route](platform-workflow.md#10-landing-product-feeds-and-webmaster), choose the relevant products and prepare corrections plus campaign material. The supported [product-ad route](current-actions.md#create-a-feed-driven-product-ad) uploads a valid retained YML RETAIL feed and creates one PAUSED OFFER ShoppingAd in a selected ЕПК group, with real processing/identity readback. Prepare the supported PAUSED campaign first when needed; then complete this product-ad step in the same task. Other profiles and filter editing retain their actual capability limits.

For regional expansion, prepare the proposed region split, local offer/landing changes, known service restrictions and overlap with the original; resolve provider region receipts at the plan stage.

For account transfer, discover the permitted source and target accounts from the existing catalog. `copy_campaign` in `propose_operations` reads the selected source campaign itself; provide `source_account_id`, its selected `source_native_scope_proof_id`, `source_campaign_id` and a fresh target `campaign_id` alias. Use the target account's `creator_account` scope and analysis basis. Set `strategy` to the observed source strategy: MAX_CLICKS, MAX_CONVERSIONS, TARGET_CPA or static-value TARGET_ROAS. The supported SEARCH/NETWORK plain Responsive profile preserves complete groups, exact phrases/autotargeting settings, campaign/group negatives, geography, title/text alternatives, href/UTM and campaign tracking/schedule. Do not manually rebuild this supported graph or reuse source native IDs as target IDs.

For a source counter/event, use the **target account's** `get_yandex_measurement_catalog` and `select_yandex_measurement_scope` route above to establish its own current access. Select the matching site/event yourself from the actual definitions and known business meaning; the user need not find provider IDs. If the original counter/goal is accessible and intended in the target, omit a mapping to preserve its identity. Otherwise pass the explicitly selected `target_optimization={counter_id,goal_id}` for a conversion strategy. Source CPA, BidCeiling, integer Crr and static goal value are preserved. An omitted `target_optimization.goal_value_rub` retains the source value; supply it only for an intentionally changed static estimate, with known basis and optional `value_evidence_ref`. The frozen recipe shows both source and target measurement settings and target owner proofs. Neither a mapped definition nor a copied static value proves business equivalence, observed revenue or CRM ROAS. For MAX_CLICKS with one analytic CounterIds and no PriorityGoals, select a counter-only scope (`goal_id` omitted); optional `target_counter_id` maps that counter without inventing a conversion goal. Source access never proves target access.

Backend rejects extra counters, multiple or unmatched priority goals, special strategy GoalId 12/13, dynamic `IsMetrikaSourceOfValue=YES`, exploration budgets, positive/unknown automatic budget adaptation, unsupported assets/extensions, shared negative sets, audiences, modifiers and other placements before writes. Native `BudgetIncreasePercent=0` on target CPA/ROAS means adaptation is disabled and is preserved. Preserve useful inventory and explain the specific remaining gap. The frozen plan uses the explicitly selected finite target money-policy period; historical source dates do not renew. Execute the same accepted plan to create PAUSED objects, then read its Application; moderation and paid delivery remain separate. A preparation-only request stops before execution. Preserve exact replay after a lost response; never create another copy to resolve an unknown add. Transfer does not require an unrelated package or Work save.

With an automatic strategy, Direct owns keyword bids. Source `Bid`/`ContextBid` remain observations in the copied recipe; they are not active manual bid instructions and are not promised to match in the target. Preserve the approved strategy, its ceiling and finite budget. [Provider semantics](https://yandex.ru/dev/direct/doc/ru/keywords/add).

For a request to return visitors or exclude recent achievers, prepare the exact event/windows and use [retargeting attachment](current-actions.md#retargeting-windows-and-exclusions) with the selected non-serving NETWORK group and current goal proof. The parent must be actually `SUSPENDED` or exact native `OFF` + `DRAFT`; the latter remains `INACTIVE`/`NOT_SUBMITTED` in normalized readback. An ordinary Creator group's active criteria require the explicit `replace_group_targeting=true` conversion, with its stops visible in the Plan; the default never silently removes them. Freeze and verify the separate attachment before offering paid launch; it neither submits moderation nor resumes delivery. Retain any concrete group/applicability gap in the same Work.
