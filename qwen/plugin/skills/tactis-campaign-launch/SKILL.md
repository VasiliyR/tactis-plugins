# Tactis — создание и запуск кампаний

Deliver the requested stage from the known offer and constraints. Use [entry](../tactis/references/session-entry.md) only for missing scope or a consequential goal choice. Reuse selected copy, suitable agreed goals and relevant [prior learning](../tactis/references/prior-learning.md).

Prepare [a coherent recommendation](../tactis/references/campaign-launch.md#recommend-the-campaign): purpose, audience/channel, geography, complete material, defensible budget/time proposal and measurement. Own these choices; ask only for a material business restriction that known context cannot resolve. Finish independent preparation while a required choice or access remains open.

For a material draft, read [the material stage](../tactis/references/campaign-launch.md#construct-the-first-usable-material). Give intent groups or the supported NETWORK structure, complete copy, destination, applicable keywords/negatives and known settings. Ground promises in the offer or attributed page evidence. Missing measured demand leaves an estimate, not measured frequency.

| Requested stage | Result to establish |
| --- | --- |
| Prepare material | Complete usable recommendation and copy, with unresolved conditions |
| Create a campaign | A supported graph created PAUSED, with actual new IDs and per-object readback |
| Moderate | The exact submitted objects and actual moderation/readiness state |
| Start paid delivery | The authorized finite launch, actual serving state and agreed observation condition |

When constructing or continuing a provider plan, read [campaign preparation and launch](../tactis/references/campaign-launch.md) for the exact capability, geography, financial and measurement prerequisites, and the relevant [current actions](../tactis/references/current-actions.md) section for proposal/execution/recovery. Creation and moderation do not authorize paid delivery. Preserve current rights, exact scope and applicable user choices; resolve an unknown external outcome under the existing operation identity before another effect.

Continue through the stages already authorized while their prerequisites hold. Return the actual stage and useful material, or the exact remaining blocker. Queued, partial and unknown states remain visible. Provider eligibility, observed impressions, mature results and causal benefit are different claims.

Use [the save stage](../tactis/references/durable-continuation.md#save-the-result-and-next-step) for requested persistence or a selected result that needs continuation. Resume the existing material and Plan/Application rather than creating a second campaign.
