# Useful tasks and capability discovery

Use this guide for «Что умеет Tactis?», examples of useful work, and choosing an
open first task. Describe the user's result, the inputs that matter and one next
step. Do not explain the MCP tool inventory, twelve product names or internal
versions to an ordinary user. The catalogue groups 34 new tasks into 14 needs;
continuing selected saved work is a separate utility.

## General explanation without a connection

Answer a general product question from this local guide. Do not call
`get_plugin_update_status`, `list_projects`, ask for OAuth or force client
selection just to explain capabilities. Several representative examples are
usually sufficient; expand only the areas the user asks about. Preserve an
explicit request for the full catalogue.

Useful first examples are: choose a monthly growth priority and prepare its first
material; write a complete advertising offer; review a page and prepare its first
edit. These can start from supplied business facts, offer or page text without
Direct. Supplied statistics support an attributed provisional analysis. A useful
chat answer does not prove that a file, saved result, source capture, publication
or external effect exists.

If Tactis is not installed or authorized, produce the supported part from supplied
inputs and state the specific limit when it matters. Do not pretend to save it in
Tactis. Installation, human sign-in and a host's separate authorization are needed
when the user wants connected data or durable server work. Never request tokens
in chat. General discovery creates no draft or grant before setup.

## Connected first choice

When the user wants work on their client, use [session entry](session-entry.md)
once and reuse that resolved context. Keep a handoff's explicit project-only
scope, exact advertiser or selected saved work. A cabinet account selector does
not override the original source or artifact owner.

For an open first task offer **up to three** results that fit the known business
and inputs. Start with the useful supported result now when the user has already
chosen it. Do not require every source or a full business questionnaire. Examples:

- Business description, no advertising source: a complete offer, one page edit or
  a budget scenario with assumptions. Ask only for the missing input that changes
  the result.
- Current Direct evidence: a weekly diagnosis and first improvement; preparation
  and actual advertising changes remain separate.
- Selected CRM or saved work: inquiry quality, a supported repeat-purchase rule,
  or continue the exact chosen result. Read its source or artifact before reusing
  conclusions.

Readiness belongs to the exact task, source and operation. A parent connector
marked ready does not establish another account's data. `connected` may have no
retained first publication. `ready` still needs freshness, coverage and the exact
owner read. Unknown, partial, revoked, unavailable and denied are not ready.
Use returned secure `settings_url` for setup; do not invent account or source IDs.

Work, material/report creation, existing history, source captures, publication and
provider changes have different permission checks. `own_access.work_write_allowed`
does not determine all material/report rights. Check the actual method and owner;
subscription expiry does not erase retained readable history. An active trial is
usable access. A blocked advertiser does not block authorized project-only work.

## Saved result and next step

Once a result exists, explain its concrete content and one relevant next step:
choose the complete text; review its exact change plan; share the selected version;
read feedback; check actual effect; or continue that work later. Do not always
attach a report, schedule or campaign. Follow [shared persistence](invariants.md)
and [durable continuation](durable-continuation.md), retaining original scope,
versions, business generation and full chosen material. Intermediate work remains
pending. A general catalogue answer alone needs no duplicate Work.

For «Продолжи выбранную работу» use its exact Work/material/source locators and
read the complete original result before acting. This utility is not a new task
that discards the old selection. Another host authorizes independently and reads
the same server artifacts; copied chat text or credentials do not prove access.
