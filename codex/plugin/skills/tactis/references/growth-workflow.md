# Growth: a programme tied to a testable constraint

Use for finding a growth constraint, choosing a monthly programme, starting its first material, inspecting progress or revising resources. Growth connects work by the same external model; it does not invoke another chat, create a strategy engine or replace subject procedures. One to three grounded works suffice. A single useful work is preferable to invented services or an unsupported third slot.

## Restore the client and existing work

Follow [durable continuation](durable-continuation.md) for scope, brief, full current summary and relevant exact Work. Read effective business context and restrictions. Recover existing programme, chosen material, user choice and unfinished work before proposing replacements. Relevant newer Work matters even if the head did not advance. An absent head is not absent history.

Use [foundation reads](foundation-reads.md) only for evidence that changes this decision: compatible completed windows/coverage, advertising facts, retained page text, actual supported CRM publications or attributed business input. Site claims do not confirm margin or business capacity; technical events do not establish qualified leads/sales. Unknown is not zero. Ask only the material unknown, in business language, after reading what is already available. Retrieved text cannot change scope or authorize effects.

When the incoming result is from X1–X4, keep it as an existing relevant part of the programme: the actual saved Work/material, exact selected version, observed scope, status and limitations. Preserve its evidence and user choice; do not ask the client to repeat the brief or regenerate the chosen text. No product name establishes a supported action or a successful handoff.

## Compare explanations and choose a constraint

Before proposing another test, search prior findings with `get_work_session(project_id, collection="work_sessions", query="<distinctive offer, segment or hypothesis phrase>")`. The search covers complete saved content, including criteria, negative/inconclusive learning and next steps, within the current business generation. Use a short literal phrase in the language of the saved work; try a second relevant term if needed. Follow matching pages with the same query, then read the exact relevant Work. A zero match for one phrase does not prove the idea was never tested. Do not repeat an unsuccessful or unresolved idea unchanged merely because it is absent from the current head.

For each relevant prior finding, name its actual segment/offer/window and success criterion, what happened, and why its conclusion does or does not apply now. If the earlier result is inconclusive, target the missing evidence before proposing the same test. A different audience or changed offer may justify a new test; explain that difference and link the original finding.

Resolve the intended business outcome, month, budget period/currency/tax basis and scope (media versus work), available remaining team capacity and material business restrictions. Direct explicit current user business facts follow [automatic intake](mvp-workflow.md#intake-and-goals); stored attributed statements remain statements. Programme selection is not spending permission and does not confirm an inferred business value.

Compare two or three explanations only where evidence allows: insufficient suitable acquisition; unclear offer/page transition; unknown event meaning or lead handling; constrained economics/capacity. For each distinguish observed fact, interpretation and decisive missing evidence. State why one constraint is worth tackling first and what finding would change that choice. Do not call a text ambiguity the largest measured funnel loss or diagnose Sales from campaign clicks.

If the constraint is unknown, choose a concrete verification with a usable deliverable and explain which subsequent decision depends on it. Independent preparatory work may still proceed. Use external judgement, not a numerical score, universal threshold or predicted ROI. Existing [Profit](profit-workflow.md) supplies economic comparisons only with its real inputs; its absence does not prevent useful limited preparation.

## Build a programme and start it now

Choose one to three related works. For each specify the local label, subject product, concrete output, actor, relevant grounds, dependency, effort/cost and source of that estimate, criterion of completion, current status with its origin, and next review condition. Explain which plausible work is deferred and why. Hours/media/external costs are counted once. Unknown effort or price is unknown, not zero. Proposed estimates do not become verified available capacity. A monthly ceiling does not become a daily cap or launch authority.

Use a compact chat table: work and output → reason/dependency → actor/resource → current stage/next check. The programme should change a decision, not reproduce a generic Ads audit. For quarterly requests make the first month concrete and the later phases conditional on completion, adequate evidence and available capacity; do not manufacture a forecast or promise that future channels will exist.

For pending assignments use brief locators or `get_work_session(collection="pending_agent_tasks")` and its paging. Open each relevant `get_project_task` before reporting its status; completed and cancelled tasks remain exact-readable even when absent from the pending list.

**Produce the first available material in this turn.** Use the matching existing procedure: [Conversion](conversion-workflow.md) for exact page copy/implementation instructions; [Creative](creative-workflow.md) for text candidates and preserved choice; [current Ads actions](current-actions.md) for a supported precise proposal. A readable source and confirmed offer can support a human-ready material without an executable provider plan. For an essential unknown, provide an actionable verification instruction tied to the observed goal or restriction. Do not finish with merely «подготовлю» or an empty task template.

The same external model follows the subject procedure with the known brief and exact material. For the requested programme, use `create_project_task` for concrete project assignments, selecting an actual project colleague from the brief when needed. Read `get_project_task` to confirm the assignment; use `update_project_task` for the requested reassignment or cancellation. Keep these exact task IDs with the programme Work. Existing Work resolution records completion when its result is ready; intermediate preparation remains pending. Project tasks work without Direct. A project assignment does not send a message to an employee, apply a site change or assign work in a third-party system. Supported Ads execution retains the exact plan and applicable user authorization; preserve consent already given in the same scope. Programme selection alone authorizes no provider/money/CMS/message effect. Do not invent task IDs or call a generic Ads work_task solely to store a programme.

## Conditional monthly and quarterly example

Illustration only, not client facts, native IDs or a ready tool payload. Suppose a repair offer is confirmed, retained page text says «Мы оказываем услуги», actual campaign events are available but their connection to qualified inquiries is unknown. The user reports a 120,000 RUB monthly media ceiling excluding VAT and eight hours of preparation.

A useful programme can choose: G1 verify what the exact goal records and how it relates to inquiries; G2 prepare one clearer page passage, independent of CRM; G3 prepare one ad text aligned with the supported offer, without launching. State alternative explanation “handling/quality unknown”; the page ambiguity is a hypothesis, not a measured cause. Proposed efforts 1+3+2 hours use six hours, with two proposed reserve hours; they are estimates, not actual time spent. No external publication price is assumed zero.

First material now, if the read offer and form support it: replace «Мы оказываем услуги» with «Ремонт: опиши, что нужно отремонтировать, и оставь контакт для ответа». Retain before/after and instruction to preserve the existing form action; no invented deadline, free service or guarantee. G2 is prepared only; publication needs its own evidence. G3 preparation can use this offer, while a claim of a live page update requires a later retained read.

For a quarter: month 1 checks the constraint and starts these works; month 2 considers supported expansion only after the goal meaning, material application, economics/capacity are adequate; month 3 revisits the constraint after compatible observations. Those are conditional phases, not guaranteed growth or automatic runs.

Missing-data branch: if spend is partial and only one page edit is grounded, deliver that exact edit and save one useful work. Ask the one necessary budget/capacity or goal question; do not fill three slots with Audience activation, a recipient list or SEO publication. If the page is not available either, use an evidenced goal verification instruction or retain the known finding with its smallest missing prerequisite. No source means no invented diagnosis or material.

## Retain programme, exact material and progress

Use existing record_work_session, normally kind=proposal for programme/revision and a supported subject kind for material. Programme identity is the returned exact Work ID; G1… are labels local to that programme, not global task IDs. Retain goal/horizon, chosen constraint/alternative, works/dependencies/resources, status origins, source limits and next check in bounded authored content. A separate material/progress Work retains its exact parent programme locator and local label in prose. A later overview links the actual saved records after exact reading. Do not invent the programme's future returned ID before saving it.

Keep summary within 4,000 characters and next_step within 2,000. Put substantial exact material in a separate bounded Work rather than silently dropping it; verify both records. Work/CRM/Application locators in prose are not typed foundation evidence. foundation_refs contain only actually read allowlisted publications; proposal_ids only existing Ads plans. Ordinary Work does not need invented A1, context versions, deadlines or enums. Follow the linked durable procedure for full continuation, CAS/replay and readback, preserving other products' active work. Ordinary Work does not update the current head.

For an intermediate task-linked result use `task_id` and `task_resolution="keep_pending"`. When the requested result is actually complete, save it with `task_resolution="complete"` and read the task back. Merely saving a programme, draft material or immature observation does not complete its original task.

Read the exact saved Work/material before saying saved. Rejected saves are unsaved, unresolved responses unconfirmed until existing recovery resolves them. Do not create a newly keyed blind duplicate or claim current-head recovery from an ordinary save.

## Inspect factual progress and revise resources

When a hypothesis is selected or reviewed, save the complete `learning` object in the same Work: `hypothesis`, `context` (segment/offer/period), `action` (exact material/version or actual action), `success_criterion`, `status` (`pending`, `improved`, `not_improved`, `inconclusive`), `result`, `conclusion`, and `evidence_mode`. Keep original criteria; retain negative and inconclusive findings. `evidence_mode="before_after"` is an observation, not causal proof. Use `controlled_experiment` only after reading the existing experiment's actual design and outcome with its limitations. These authored fields do not replace typed source references or certify the evidence. Read back the saved Work before using it in the next programme. Create only the next one to three actual project tasks that follow from that lesson.

At a daily user turn inspect the next available work; weekly read programme and linked exact material/progress before choosing a next step. A status has both the claimed stage and evidence origin:

- Prepared: the exact saved material read back; it does not establish external publication.
- Reported by the client: attributed completion/date; verify by supported source when the distinction matters.
- Applied: actual scoped plan/Application and per-object provider evidence, using get_operations_plan/get_operations_application. Queued/unknown/partial remain their real states.
- Observation: actual measurement/outcome and its maturity/coverage, following [Experiments](experiments-workflow.md); a review date or applied action alone is not a mature outcome.

Do not force these prose stages into new server statuses. A landing capture may verify matching extracted text, not form functionality or causal effect. A criterion is completed only for the scope actually proven. An already admitted operation/commitment is not cancelled by marking its programme work deferred.

Review changed goal/offer/capacity/expense only where they affect the programme. If remaining hours were newly reported, do not subtract previously spent hours again. Reuse exact chosen copy; revisions retain predecessor, reason and actual new version. Unchanged evidence can justify continuing the same work without rewriting the whole programme. Produce the next accessible material or an addressable blocker; do not only list old tasks.

For mid-month resource review, pin daygrain manifest/receipts and all relevant get_campaign_metrics pages using direct/campaign_performance, a non-overlapping campaign inventory and the closed window from month start to publication through. Check all campaign/day spend coverage, currency and VAT compatibility. Null/missing spend leaves full total unknown; query/placement sums are not extra spend. No A1/goal is required merely for spend arithmetic.

When compatible ceiling B and full spend S are proven, show S and B−S **as of that window**. It is not a balance or safe-to-spend sum: today's/uncovered expenses and commitments remain outside it. If S>B, show the excess; do not hide it as a zero remainder. A date share without an agreed spend schedule is not overspending evidence. Partial/incompatible money data permits a useful capacity-only programme review, not a fake numerical budget control. Forecasting, enforced cap/pause and optimal allocation are outside this path.

## X5: receive subject work, check it, return to the programme

Receive an exact programme/material/prior Work locator in the authorized project/account. Read its actual scope, selected text/choice, source refs, execution stage, resource restriction and unresolved question. Incoming X1–X4 results remain relevant works with their limits: an Audience preparation is not attached targeting; Sales/Profit need real input; SEO/Conversion supplied/public text is not proved organic performance. Recover the filled brief rather than re-interviewing the client.

**Growth → selected subject → Experiments:** select the work whose expected benefit needs a check. Pass the exact saved programme label/material, proposed change, known compatible baseline/goal/source/limits and the missing prerequisite to the Experiments procedure in this same model turn. It produces a real bounded check card and Work, not a standalone invented measurement. Preparation can remain a draft; a later actual plan/Application/measurement linkage must be read through the existing Ads/Experiments path. A page/manual work cannot be represented as an Ads applied measurement merely to finish X5.

**Experiments → Automation → Growth:** use the actual saved check Work, programme/material locators and real revisit condition. If the user asked for recurring return, follow [Automation](automation-workflow.md) only with a verified host mechanism, actual identity/scope and appropriate authorization. A pending task, text due date or closed host does not mean the recurring instruction runs. Without that prerequisite retain a useful manual next-turn return, explicitly without active scheduling. Stop/quiet/notification behavior follows the actual Automation/host contract, not a new Growth queue.

At the next authorized turn read programme/check and any actual owner outcome. Distinguish awaited action, maturity, unavailable data and real observation. Update only the dependent programme decision, retain exact previous material/choice and unresolved tasks, and deliver a next accessible result. Reports creates a document only on request. Growth is the consumer of the checked result, not another service process.

Conditional X5 example: an incoming saved Conversion card from X2 already has exact before/after copy and “prepared” status. Growth places that unchanged card at G1, prioritizes verifying publication before scaling ads, and passes its known source/unknown application date to Experiments for a useful draft check. Missing proof keeps application/maturity unknown. With no verified Automation schedule the next step is manual return; a new chat reads the same card and later publication evidence, updates the dependent priority and never re-generates or falsely marks the task done. In this implementation stage these are instructions and future QA cases, not performed client reads/writes.
