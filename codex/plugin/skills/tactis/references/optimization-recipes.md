# Ten ordinary advertising symptoms: check, decide, continue

Enter at the symptom the user named. Use the five-stage [performance diagnosis](performance-diagnosis.md) for scope, comparable windows, coverage and the saved result; this table selects its **one relevant drill-down**. It is not a requirement to run ten checks, fetch all candidates or start an account audit. Shared authority/save rules live in [invariants](invariants.md); exact collection and paging live in [foundation reads](foundation-reads.md).

## Toolbox and short route

1. Reuse authorized project/account and a pinned Daygrain/Analysis/Dossier. Otherwise `get_account_read_grant` → `get_current_daygrain` → needed `get_campaign_metrics` pages establishes the change. Keep exact event, denominator, units/VAT, attribution, window and coverage.
2. Choose one row below. Configuration: `get_current_configuration` → scoped `get_configuration_campaign`. Detailed observations: completed `get_dossier_manifest` → `get_campaign_dossier` → needed `get_dossier_family` pages; `capture_analysis`/`capture_dossiers` only if that relevant frozen material does not exist. Follow jobs rather than treating admission as observations.
3. Return object → observed change → competing explanation/unknown → ready check or exact proposal → revisit condition. Save/read back through `record_work_session`/`get_work_session`. A supported change uses [current actions](current-actions.md), preserving freeze → chat choice → execute → readback.

| Symptom | Three checks in order, with actual source | Useful result or honest unknown |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. CPA rose | (1) `get_campaign_metrics`: identify the exact denominator, compare mature equal windows and coverage. (2) Decompose compatible spend/event changes; `get_analysis_manifest` and `list_analysis_campaigns` can supply owner economics. (3) Inspect the largest known contributor's relevant dossier family; paid-sale meaning/linkage goes to [Sales](sales-workflow.md) | One priority grounded in the price/volume/event change. A technical-event CPA is labelled as such; incompatible Direct/Metrika or unknown paid linkage leaves business CPA unknown |
| 2. Spend rose without more results | (1) Same-window spend and selected event. (2) `get_configuration_campaign(family="campaign")` budget/strategy and exact saved Plan/Application if a change occurred. (3) Query/placement mix in the relevant dossier, or [Profit](profit-workflow.md) for business limits | Explain the observed extra spend and first check. No recommendation to cut/scale solely from a ratio or current setting |
| 3. Impressions/clicks fell or stopped | (1) Coverage/verified zero from metrics. (2) Campaign settings/status, then only affected `ads`/`keywords` families. (3) Exact Plan/Application or refresh due time when relevant | Named status/eligibility/collection check. A captured SUSPENDED state does not establish when or why delivery fell; shortage of demand, eligibility and budget remain alternatives |
| 4. CTR fell | (1) Compatible clicks/impressions, same population. (2) SEARCH query mix, or РСЯ placements/devices; inspect only the changed mix. (3) Exact scoped `ads` and grounded [Creative](creative-workflow.md) material | Concrete message/mix check or draft. Low CTR alone does not prove poor leads, creative fatigue or a winning replacement |
| 5. Clicks remain; website events fell | (1) Exact selected event and counter definition via `get_audience_sources`; covered visits/events separately. (2) `get_page_observation` when a matching retained URL/device observation exists. (3) Exact ad URL and `get_landing_page(kinds=["title","heading","text"])` for message/destination | Tracking or landing assignment with exact page/event. Page text does not prove a functioning form; missing/late events are not zero conversions |
| 6. SEARCH spends on irrelevant queries | (1) `get_dossier_family(family="search_query")` and/or `query_observations`, preserving goal/coverage. (2) Classify observed intent against actual offer; inspect current `keywords` and existing negatives. (3) Review candidate `R1`/`R3`/`R10`/`D1` only when relevant | Exact query → reason → proposed negative/operator or no exclusion. Review useful matching demand; a shared token in bad queries does not make all queries containing it irrelevant |
| 7. SEARCH has useful queries missing from the offer/group | (1) Query intent and coverage. (2) Current `keywords`/`ad_groups`; relevant `R5`/`R11` or `structure` candidates. (3) Exact landing/copy support and overlap risk | Ready group/theme/keyword or copy material only within supported operations. Query success is observational; overlapping query/keyword partitions are not additive leads |
| 8. РСЯ has costly placements or device differences | (1) `get_dossier_family(family="placement")` or `device` with its denominator/coverage. (2) Relevant `R2`/`R6`/`R7` candidates plus source rows. (3) Exact goal, lag and current exclusions/modifiers | A specific placement/segment check or supported proposal. Search intent/minus rules do not diagnose РСЯ; few events, missing goal evidence or small volume leave efficiency uncertain |
| 9. Website leads look cheap; sales/quality are poor | (1) Confirm what the website event means. (2) `get_crm_source_status` → exact `get_crm_source_publication`, or selected human-confirmed `get_crm_publications`. (3) Check qualification/payment definition, cutoff, cohort and campaign linkage | Known quality/paid groups plus one [Sales assignment](sales-workflow.md). Unlinked account totals cannot condemn a campaign; current stages cannot prove response SLA or a loss reason |
| 10. Budget cannot be spent, or scaling stopped working | (1) Spend/traffic and mature outcomes, separating SEARCH/РСЯ. (2) Current campaign strategy/budget/eligibility; known capacity and [Profit constraints](profit-workflow.md#budget-proposal-under-business-constraints). (3) For a real demand question, retained `get_demand_publication` and explicitly assumed `preview_demand_scenario`; avoid repeating overlapping phrase counts | Feasible bounded budget/strategy check, not an invented saturation curve. Historical average CPA and search demand do not predict the next sale or prove a provider spending ceiling |

Candidate IDs/families refer to `get_candidate_manifest` and paged `get_candidate_family` of the selected exact Candidate publication, never the Daygrain ID. The catalog exposes R1–R7, R10–R11, D1, structure, creative and efficiency; **there are no R8/R9 tools to invent**. Heuristics neither replace raw evidence nor authorize execution.

## Sufficient-fixture call budget

For a complete preauthorized benchmark with known project/account, binding, exact event and a small one-page campaign scope, two completed weeks can use four data calls: `get_current_daygrain`, Direct weekly `get_campaign_metrics`, exact-event weekly `get_campaign_metrics`, and one already-known relevant configuration/dossier reader. At most two additional addressed reads can resolve the selected check (≤6 data calls). Work persistence is measured separately. This target assumes enough rows/coverage and preexisting needed locators; it is not a universal six-call limit. Missing scope, jobs, pagination or event definitions require their real calls and remain visible. Do not omit a necessary page to meet a budget.
